## Frame

**A (AE101): agentic engineering is engineering — a feedback-controlled loop whose checks, not its voice, decide; what you encode compounds.** 95.
- M3 the-loop-half-filled: "The agent loop is a **closed-loop controller**... Cut the feedback signal (a test, a check, a read) and it drifts."
- M5 what-packaging-is: "**A check is a position fix**. At a fix the wedge of possible states collapses to a point."
- M6 composing-the-workflow: "An **eval** is a measurement... One run tells you what happened. The measurement tells you what the system does."
- Where it breaks: M5 what-packaging-is "Whether this field ever settles into a real best practice is an open question. Either way, today's playbooks are **candidates**." and M5 the-gate-is-a-claim "Today's right procedure, your gates and workflow, yours or the agent's, is superseded too."
M2 (plan read as calibration), M4 (un-packaged send-off as experiment) and M6 (eval) only make sense through it.

**B (APT101): building got cheap, deciding didn't — the trio's job is the outcome, the bet that can lose and the reading of the signal.** 92.
- D1 apt101-building-is-cheap: "Agents make the slow step cheap. The hard steps stay where they were... None of those got cheaper."
- D2 apt101-why-it-agreed: "It analysed everything and still had no insight... Its headline is your own look-for line handed back, not an insight."
- D3 apt101-three-jobs-rewritten: "Your insight is the choice it leaves you with... That is real strategy work, and it does not get cheaper when building does."
- Where it breaks, on the page: D3 apt101-where-you-go-from-here "This is where 'building got cheap, deciding didn't' stops being an edge on its own... What would change this training's mind: agents that start deciding well."
Short of A: the Day 2 checking block (catch-it-making-things-up, fluent-is-not-true) is reached through the frame only via "deciding"; read alone it is AE101's verification frame ported in. The braid is "A team that writes for its own criteria has rebuilt the feature factory with a dashboard" (D3 apt101-what-good-means), which arrives late.

## Narrative

**A.** 94. An engineer wants the agent to do real work unwatched. M1–M3 build a kit on short loops; M4 sends the long task off un-packaged ("send it off plainly and you find out what you can't steer yet", M4 set-the-markers-send-it-off). The turn: it comes back underdelivering, and that is the lesson ("The un-packaged run was supposed to underdeliver. What came back is data, not blame.", M5 diagnose-and-resend). It is re-sent packaged and the two runs are diffed. Then the training turns the failure on itself: "The agent I was working with had just finished writing those three modules. It still opened with the un-packaged shape." (M6 story-of-module-6). The turn is the training's own failure, and it sits close to the 100 anchor.

**B.** 90. A trio arrives with the last thing they built that nobody asked for ("what were you sure of at the time?", D1 Start here). Day 1 they paint a box, write a bet that can lose, and brief a digest in their own words, with the expectation kept "outside the brief so the run never reads it" (D1 apt101-send-it-off). The turn comes on Day 2: the digest found what each person asked for. It is lived first, through four lines side by side and the reversed rerun ("find what in my material argues against", D2 apt101-read-the-digest Phase 4), and named afterwards ("The headline came from your question as much as from your customers.", D2 apt101-why-it-agreed). Day 3: five users touch or break each person's own Day 1 line ("Your own line, not someone else's.", D3 apt101-five-users). The close asks the opening question again of their own Monday plan ("is anything in `team/monday.md` the next thing your team builds that nobody asked for?", D3 Say it to each other). The turn is lived on every seat. What keeps it from 100: the training's own failure is the guide's (an installer, a sharing failure), not the training's on its own surface. One small leak: D2 read-the-digest Phase 3 asks "Did your line ask for support, a test, or a frame?" before the rerun, which half-names the turn.

## Room (B)

The exercises carry the story. The turn is produced on the trio's own material before any lecture names it, and the close is a room beat.

**Strongest:**
1. D1 apt101-send-it-off Phase 4 → D2 apt101-read-the-digest Phases 1–4. **Weakest:**
