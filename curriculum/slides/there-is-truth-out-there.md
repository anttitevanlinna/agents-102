## There is truth out there
<!--slide:there-is-truth-out-there-->

There is truth out there. Your sources carry shards of it. Your agent, left to itself, has no model of truth, only a model of what usually comes next in language that looks like yours.

When your sources support a request, the output is grounded. When they don't, the agent still produces something, and the tone looks the same.

Grounded means traceable to a specific file, number or quote in a source. A grounded claim can still be wrong if its source is wrong. Start with grounded.
