## Your taste is the ceiling
<!--slide:your-taste-is-the-ceiling-->

Ask an agent for a screen and you get a screen. Ask for a screen for the customer who nearly left last spring, built around the sentence she said in the interview, and you get a different screen.

The agent mirrors what you bring. A vague ask gets a confident, average answer. A sharp ask, with your judgement in it, gets something worth arguing about.

Making got cheap. Knowing what good looks like did not.
