PROTO A101-06 THE FULL AGENT PICTURE · SELECTION The loop runs inside the runtime and does one thing: make more than one, pick, write down what won. Context and tools sit outside the loop; the wall cuts through them.

Idea

The loop is the mechanism the closing lecture names, drawn inside the runtime: the model makes more than one candidate, the checks let one through, and what survived is written into context as memory.
Context is both where the loop reads and where it writes, so the next pass starts from what was kept; tools are reached from outside the loop, and the goal is what the pick is judged against.
The give / keep back decision is the boundary itself: the wall cuts through the tools and the context, and what sits outside it is what you kept back.

What it cannot show

The order the pieces were added in; the figure is the finished arrangement.
That the checks come in two kinds (floor and ceiling), and that other agents can sit among the tools or be the generator; both collapse into one gate and one model.
That the interface also carries the goal in and the result out; it is drawn only as the door where a person meets it.

Where it breaks

One gate with one gap implies exactly one winner per pass; real checks often pass none, or several, and sometimes hand back to you instead.
The model reaches tools and context many times inside a single pass, which one arrow each undersells.
