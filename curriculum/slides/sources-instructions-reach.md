## The instructions are one suspect; your question is another
<!--slide:sources-instructions-reach-->

When a digest misleads you, the urge is to rewrite the agent's instructions. They are one suspect. Check all the suspects before changing anything.

- The material. A stale export, a missing interview, two sources that disagreed and got blended. Fix the material.
- The instructions. One agent doing two jobs, or themes asked for but never how many customers said each one. Fix the instructions.
- Your question. Asked to find evidence for a hypothesis, an agent finds evidence for it. Ask what argues against it too.

Ask the agent to make this diagnosis first. Then rerun the smallest step that should now behave differently, not the whole night.
