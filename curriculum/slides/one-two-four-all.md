## Hear every idea before anything gets averaged
<!--slide:one-two-four-all-->

Ask an agent to merge everyone's ideas and it tends to hand back the middle. So hear every idea first, written down with a name on it.

1-2-4-All, from Liberating Structures, takes about a quarter of an hour. One minute alone, in silence, writing ideas down. Two minutes in pairs, building on them while keeping each idea distinct. Five minutes in fours, refining them and telling them apart. Then all together, where a few people share something everyone should hear.

The numbers are group sizes, not minutes. Its authors, Henri Lipmanowicz and Keith McCandless, say it "engages every voice regardless of group size".
