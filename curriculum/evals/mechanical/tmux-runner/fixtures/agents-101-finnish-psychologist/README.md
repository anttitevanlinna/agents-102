# Synthetic persona kit: Finnish psychologist

Everything in this fixture is fictional. Salla Niemelä is a solo psychologist
in Finland. She runs a private practice, a small website, a newsletter, and
professional social accounts. She is also writing a general-audience book.

Salla uses an LLM assistant, currently Claude, to outline and draft public
content. She does not use it for diagnosis, treatment, client communication,
session notes, or any other client material. The scenario must preserve that
boundary: her profession does not itself make ordinary publishing assistance a
high-risk AI use.

Her M2 challenge is to build a proportionate EU AI Act readiness system before
increasing her publishing cadence. The system must distinguish legal text,
operational choices, and questions for qualified counsel. It must not certify
compliance.

The deliberate evidence seam is a conflict between a fictional adviser's
blanket “label every AI-assisted item” rule and official EU material that ties
deployer transparency duties to specific uses and publication conditions. M3
adds fresh 2026 Commission guidance so the system must update rather than repeat
the loudest checklist.
