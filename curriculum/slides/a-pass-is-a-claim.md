## A pass is a claim about a check nobody has tested
<!--slide:a-pass-is-a-claim-->

When the agent's check says "good", it means it saw nothing wrong. It does not mean the work is good. Until you compare the check's verdicts with your own, you do not know how often it approves work you would have sent back.

Take a handful of real cases, judge them yourselves, and put your calls next to its verdicts. Where you disagree, add the reason to the check's instructions and run it again. In 2024, Hamel Husain, who writes on evaluating AI products, reported reaching better than ninety percent agreement on one product after three rounds of exactly this.

Asking the same agent twice in the same conversation is not a second opinion.
