## Will your organisation learn faster than the model changes underneath it?
<!--slide:learn-faster-than-the-model-->

The model gets better on its own, for free. What your company knows about its own work gets better only when somebody writes it down and an agent builds on it.

If what your company knows stays flat, every model release widens the gap.
