## Generic AI becomes yours when you shape its context
<!--slide:generic-becomes-yours-->

Ask any model about your market and you get the industry's average answer. A competitor asking the same question gets much the same one.

Shape the context around it, with your interviews, your tickets, your product box and the words your customers actually used, and the answer changes. It cites your material. It reasons from your situation. It sounds like your product, not a market report. The answer belongs to what you gave the agent, not to the model behind it.

The model has read what the field published. It has not read how your customers behave with your product. Only your team holds that.
