## Reassess the residual, then decide
<!--slide:reassess-the-residual-->

**Reassess the residual.** Name the risk that remains and write it down: *the residual risk is X; if Y happens, we made it less likely, not prevented it.*

**Decide.** Accept the residual on record, or close the door.

**"I can't tell"** is a real answer. Closing it takes evidence, not a better guess.

The discipline is running the loop, not reaching certainty.
