## Widen first, then choose, and expect to loop back
<!--slide:diverge-then-converge-->

The UK Design Council's double diamond draws two shapes that open and then close. The first is about the problem: Discover, then Define. The second is about the solution: Develop, then Deliver. Each diamond explores widely first and then narrows to focused action.

Two things the popular telling gets wrong. It is not four stages in a line. In the Council's words, "This is not a linear process": what a team learns can send it back to the beginning. And it was not invented in 2003–04. Richard Eisermann, one of that team: "What we did with the Double Diamond was codify it, rename the steps and popularise it."

Keep widening and choosing apart. An agent widens in minutes. The choosing stays with the team.
