## A missing name marks what isn't built yet
<!--slide:a-missing-name-is-a-finding-->

Write a proposal for the wider team and some parts fill fast: what the agents do, which sources they read, what runs each week. The owners stall. Who reads what the agents produce? Who corrects the agents when they get a customer wrong? Who decides what goes in?

The stall is the proposal being accurate. A part with no named owner is a part not built yet, and it is the part that decides whether any of the rest gets used.

So leave the gap visible and keep the next move small: one name, one person whose week this would change, who would tell you plainly if it did not.
