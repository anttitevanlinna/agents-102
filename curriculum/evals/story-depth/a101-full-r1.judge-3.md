## Frame

**A — AE101.** Frame in one sentence: agentic engineering is engineering, so the agent's output, its report and your own checks are all claims until something outside the session measures them, and what survives gets written down so the next session starts further on.

Three modules that only make sense through it:
- M1, `lectures/the-machine-you-just-met.md`: "A check from outside the session resets the chain." A trivial bug fix is only worth a module if the bug is the excuse for the loop.
- M4, `lectures/test-and-learn.md`: "The agent is the apparatus, your rules and context are the setup, and the result is data." Sending a task off with no plan and no verifier is malpractice under any other lens.
- M5, `lectures/the-gate-is-a-claim.md`: "The check you built is itself a claim that wants verifying." The lens is turned on the lens's own instrument.

Where it says it breaks: M2, `lectures/how-instructions-grow.md`: "This training stops short of the full system"; M5, `lectures/what-packaging-is.md`: "Whether this field ever settles into a real best practice is an open question."; M5, `lectures/the-gate-is-a-claim.md`: "Retire what the next model outgrows, add what it needs."

Anchor: clears 80 (every module reads through it) and most of 100 (the break is named three times, by mechanism). Held just short because the six-phase map is a second organising device that the student has to carry beside the lens.

**B — A101.** Frame in one sentence: an agent is a handful of plain parts (context, memory, other agents, a boundary, checks, a loop, an interface), each one a single decision about what you give it and what you keep back, and it becomes yours rather than generic by those decisions.

Three modules that only make sense through it:
- M2, `lectures/compounding.md`: "The mechanism is the same; only the shelf life changed." A folder of markdown is only a module's worth of content if memory is context with a longer life.
- M4, `lectures/practice-of-risk.md`: "More context makes a good answer likelier. Less access makes a bad answer smaller." Security is the same decision run in the other direction, and the page says so: "Now the move is to give it less."
- M6, `lectures/new-human-role-in-the-loop.md`: "You added those parts one at a time, and each one was a single decision: what you give it, and what you keep back." The send-the-mail walk re-reads five modules as one arrangement.

Where it says it breaks: M7, `lectures/access-is-not-absorption.md`: "Every piece so far was yours to decide. This one is not."; M2, `lectures/compounding.md`: "The agent can only work from what somebody wrote down."; M8, `lectures/where-is-this-all-going.md`: "Some of what you built here will be obsolete within a year."

Anchor: clears 80, and reaches for 100 — the frame is stated as a recurring "In the full agent picture" beat in every module and its limit is named where it bites (the one part with another person on the far side). Two things hold it under A. The frame reads as a parts list more often than as a lens that predicts the next module; and M1 names the arc before it is earned (`lectures/what-just-happened.md`: "The rest of the training is seven more instances of the same move.").

## Narrative

**A.** In five sentences: An engineer who already uses the tool fixes a trivial bug and is told the bug was the excuse. They learn to distrust a plausible plan, then a plausible skill grade, always with the result a few minutes away. Then the training sends them off with nothing — no plan, no verifier — and the session comes back confident and partly wrong. They read the failure, build the one check their own failure earned, and send the same task again. At the close the author admits the agent that wrote these modules made the same mistake, and so did he.

The turn, M5, `exercises/diagnose-and-resend.md`: "The un-packaged run was supposed to underdeliver. What came back is data, not blame." Set up in M4, `lectures/test-and-learn.md`: "Session one goes now, un-packaged: no plan.md, no verifier, no reference artifact." And made the training's own in M6, `lectures/story-of-module-6.md`: "The agent I was working with had just finished writing those three modules. It still opened with the un-packaged shape."

Anchor: clears 80 (the student lives the turn across two sittings) and touches 100 (the turn is also the training's own failure). The first three modules move by accumulation, not by stakes; the plot starts at M4.

**B.** In five sentences: A leader who has only chatted builds a page that sounds like them and feels the joy of it. They build a memory and a small crew of agents on a challenge they actually own, and the crew hands back an answer they cannot vouch for. Security does not cure the doubt, it deepens it: most rows say "I can't tell" and they are told certainty was never on offer. Then they measure four detectors on their own output and keep one judge that names its own blind spot, put it in a loop and walk out of the room. The last two modules take the win away twice — the thing they most want to hand a teammate will not go, and the room's final kernel was checked by nothing.

The turn, M3, `exercises/three-minds-one-synthesis.md`: "Everything you just did is the move this training teaches, run properly. It still handed you something you cannot vouch for." Held, not cured, in the M3 module file (`multi-agent-systems`): "The unease you feel about the synthesized briefing is not something to close today." The rescue is bounded on the page, M5 module file (`output-quality`): "This module is the rescue. Not full closure: your Module 3 doubt and your Module 4 residuals stay where they are." And the close returns to it, M8, `lectures/where-is-this-all-going.md`: "Hold that doubt the way you held it then."

Anchor: clears 80 — the turn is in an exercise, on the student's own briefing, and the mood arc is a real plot with a second turn at M7. It is near 100 because the turn is the training's own method failing on the page, not the student's error. It stays under A for two reasons: the plot is carried by exercises through M6 and by lectures after it (see Room), and the protagonist's arrival is conferred rather than recognised (M8, `exercises/joint-double-diamond.md`: "You are now agent builders.").

## Stance

**A.** Positions it could lose a customer over, and where each is defended:
- Published best practice is a forecast until you test it on your repo. Mechanism, M5, `lectures/what-packaging-is.md`: "A prediction is not a measurement." and "So a playbook stays a candidate until something tests it here".
- More rules is not the fix. Mechanism, M2, `lectures/how-instructions-grow.md`: "The work starts passing your rules and missing your point."
- A passing check proves little. Mechanism, M5, `lectures/the-gate-is-a-claim.md`: "Green is a claim about the check, not a fact about the work."
- Loading a rule does not make it hold. Scar, M6, `lectures/story-of-module-6.md`: "The grep pass caught each one. The LLM self-check did not."
- Earned trust makes you a worse watcher. Mechanism, M4, `lectures/ironies-of-automation.md`: "The trust is deserved. The watching still has to be engineered."

Against its own interest: it tells a room that paid for a method that the method is provisional (M5, `lectures/the-gate-is-a-claim.md`: "Today's right procedure, your gates and workflow, yours or the agent's, is superseded too.") and that the trainer is "not the debugger or necessarily the deepest Claude Code expert here" (M1 module file). Real, but it never names its own sale. Anchor: well past 80, short of the 100 line.

**B.** Positions it could lose a customer over, and where each is defended:
- You cannot share an agent. Mechanism, M7, `lectures/access-is-not-absorption.md`: "The first three copy in an afternoon. The fourth does not copy at all"; the exercise holds the line too, M7, `exercises/share-your-work.md`: "is a vendor pitch and is NOT on the list."
- Certainty is not for sale. Mechanism, M4, `lectures/practice-of-risk.md`: "Your firewall doesn't see it because your firewall has never had to worry about persuasion."
- Do not split agents, straight after a module that showed splitting working. M3, `lectures/when-to-split-an-agent.md`: "A whole module just showed you multi-agent works. Next Monday, you will be tempted to apply it to everything. Don't."
- Asking the model to check itself is not a check. Case, M4 prework `lectures/module-5-prework.md`: "Verification has to leave the generation loop and touch the source."
- Rollout numbers lie. M7, `lectures/access-is-not-absorption.md`: "The two get reported as one number and they are not one thing."
- Reading about it is not doing it. M1, `lectures/what-just-happened.md`: "Reading a McKinsey report on the agentic enterprise is not the same category of activity as what you just did."

Against its own commercial interest, on the page, twice: M4, `lectures/practice-of-risk.md`: "It costs whoever sells you agents too, this training included: an agent that reaches less is a smaller thing to sell, and it is still the right call."; and the M8 module file: "It would have demoed well and it would have been the wrong thing to sell you". That is the 100 anchor's own wording, met. Held a little under it because two positions are asserted more than argued (plain text beats every fancier setup; "the lazy story" of the human disappearing).

## The pair

**A.** Holds ground and doubts its tools in the same breath: the check you were just taught to build is "trusted on vibes" until measured, and the author's own rules leaked. Reads as someone who has been there (both).

**B.** Also both, and the doubt is more evenly spread than in A: every module from M3 on ends by declining to close something ("The unease stays. Nothing today resolves it." — M4 module file; "Nothing has been scoring the judge." — M6, `lectures/when-the-score-stops-moving.md`), while the positions above do not soften. Reads as someone who has been there — with the oddity that the page never says where "there" was. The stance has scars' worth of conviction and no scar.

## Room

For B: do the exercises carry the story, so the turn is lived and not just told?

Through M6, yes. The joy, the first unease, the deeper unease, the rescue and the leverage each happen inside an exercise, on the student's own material, and the lecture after names it ("You felt it move." — M1, `lectures/what-just-happened.md`; "You have done this before" — M5, `lectures/grounded.md`). From M7 the direction reverses: the exercises become prompt-then-read-the-file procedures and the lectures tell the student what they felt.

Two strongest exercise beats:
1. M3, `exercises/three-minds-one-synthesis.md`, the Close. The student is asked a question of their own work and denied the fix: "Hold the doubt. Name it to yourself. Don't fix it here." The whole plot hangs on this and it is in the exercise, not the lecture.
2. M6, `exercises/eval-loop.md`, the walk-away and return. The student leaves the room and the page stakes the result on something they can check: "If the judge file moved, the exercise failed." Then, flat: "And you weren't there."

(Close third: M5, `exercises/hallucination-bakeoff.md` — "Save it. **Don't open it yet.**")

Two weakest exercise beats:
1. M7, `exercises/design-the-sharing-plan.md`. The lecture claims a lived beat — "You felt this an hour ago. The technical plan filled quickly. The people plan stalled on names." (`lectures/access-is-not-absorption.md`) — but in the exercise the agent does the stalling for the student: "Missing names remain visible as `UNASSIGNED — Monday's question`; the agent does not invent them." The student's whole part is "Read the two files side by side." The turn is told, then back-dated.
2. M8, `exercises/joint-double-diamond.md`. Nine timed stages of file choreography, and the two story beats are written in advance of the room: "The forum didn't converge on the first try. Pushback rounds were the work." asserts what happened before it has, and the identity is handed over by script ("You are now agent builders."). The beat the lecture then recovers — "Now open the kernel and count how many folders it actually cites." — is the one the exercise should have ended on.
