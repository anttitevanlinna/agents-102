## c1-state (every-improvement-is-learning-that-compounds)

Predicted: +10: students already write down 'company knowledge must be written down', and this ties it to agents editing agents and to speed.. Risk: It adds a person-versus-agent comparison that some readers may take as a dig at human teams. The fixed-check caveat is kept.

Before:

> What multiplies your work is how fast you and your organisation learn. Agents that improve agents speed up that learning as far as a check measures it.

After:

> What multiplies your work is how fast you and your organisation learn. When one agent edits another, the fix is a lesson written down, and the next agent starts from it within minutes. A lesson passed from person to person takes weeks to reach the next team. Learning speeds up this way as far as a check measures it.
