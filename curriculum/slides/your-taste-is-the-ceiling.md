## Your taste is the ceiling
<!--slide:your-taste-is-the-ceiling-->

Ask an agent for a screen and you get a screen. Ask for a screen for the customer who nearly left last spring, built around the sentence she said in the interview, and you get a different screen.

The agent mirrors what you bring. Language models are tuned on what people prefer, so they lean toward agreeing with the stance in front of them. A vague ask gets a confident, average answer. A sharp ask, with your judgement in it, gets something worth arguing about.

Making got cheap. Knowing what good looks like did not. Seth Godin has argued for years that tools are cheap and taste is the scarce part; agents make that true faster.
