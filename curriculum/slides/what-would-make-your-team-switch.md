## What would have to be true for your team to switch?
<!--slide:what-would-make-your-team-switch-->

Your team already has a way of doing the work you want agents to join: a digest someone writes by hand each week, a spreadsheet of interview notes, a gut call that has worked well enough for years. Your proposal is not competing with nothing. It is competing with that.

So ask the question the other way round. Not *how do we get the team to adopt this?* but *what would have to be true for them to switch?* The answers come back small and specific. It lands where they already look. It is right the first time they try it. Someone they trust has used it and said so.
