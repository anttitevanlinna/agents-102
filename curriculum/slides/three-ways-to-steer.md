## You steer with what you bring, what you set and what you ask
<!--slide:three-ways-to-steer-->

- **Bring** a stance: a stated doubt, or a standard the answer has to clear.
- **Set** a check around it, such as a second agent with a different brief. It sits outside the conversation and catches what one pass lets through.
- **Ask** for a shape: a ranked list with a source for each line, a one-page summary, three options side by side.

Each one needs someone who knows the product.
