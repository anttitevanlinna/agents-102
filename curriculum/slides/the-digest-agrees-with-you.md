## The digest agrees with your favourite hypothesis
<!--slide:the-digest-agrees-with-you-->

A headline that says what your bet says, with a few quotes under it and a ticket count beside it, feels like good news. It is also the moment you are least likely to check, because the answer is the one you hoped for.

Read that headline as the line to check first. Which interviews are behind it, and what did the rest of your material say?
