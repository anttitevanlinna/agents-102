## Test one morning a month, with the team watching
<!--slide:a-morning-a-month-->

Steve Krug's do-it-yourself usability testing, in his 2010 book *Rocket Surgery Made Easy*, fits in one morning a month. Three users each try realistic tasks and think out loud: what they are looking at, what they are trying to do, what they are thinking. The person running the test stays neutral and does not help.

The team watches. After each session, every observer writes down the three most serious problems they saw. Over lunch you compare notes, and decide what to fix before next month. An agent can merge the notes into one list before lunch; the choosing stays with the people who watched.

Krug says he invented none of this. Jakob Nielsen was its loudest advocate, and five users is Nielsen's number. Three in a morning is Krug's plan.
