## An opportunity tree on Friday, a feature roadmap on Monday
<!--slide:tree-on-friday-roadmap-on-monday-->

The workshop ends with a tree: the outcome at the top, the customer needs and pains that stand in its way, a handful of solutions under them. Everyone leaves agreeing.

On Monday the roadmap arrives as a list of features with dates. The tree stays on the wall.

The team was not careless. Testing which branch was right cost a sprint per branch, and the roadmap already owned the sprints. So the decision went to whatever was already planned, and the tree became a picture of what the team believed.
