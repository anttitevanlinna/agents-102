## It found what you asked it to look for
<!--slide:it-found-what-you-asked-->

The agent's brief, written while the hypothesis was fresh, said: find evidence that customers stall at the import step. It did. It searched your interviews and tickets for exactly that, and it found some.

Ask an agent to look for something in enough customer conversations and it will usually find a few. What it was never asked is how many customers said something else, or what would show the hypothesis wrong.

So the agreement may come from your question, not from your customers. Ask again the other way round: what in this material argues against our hypothesis, and how much of it is there?
