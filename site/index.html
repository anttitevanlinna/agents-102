Where it starts

First something that is unmistakably you

You learn what an agent can do on work you know better than anyone. Then you turn it on your real challenge.

Prework

A game and your week

Getting Going

A personal site that is you

Building Agent Systems

A memory around your challenge

Name the challenge you are carrying at work. The agents gather your sources into a memory they keep current. Every later module works on it.

How it works

Keep the handbook open beside Claude

Each step says what you are about to make. You type a plain sentence into Claude, and the handbook tells you what to look for in the reply. No coding.

Type in Claude Desktop

List my main meetings this week. Summarize the shape of the week in 5 lines.

In Claude's reply

Your own meetings, read from your calendar or from a screenshot of it, and five lines on what kind of week it is.

What you need

  • Claude Pro or a higher paid plan. Pro is the minimum. The free plan is not enough.
  • Claude Desktop on a Mac or Windows computer.
  • A challenge you carry at work that isn't solved yet. Not a task.
  • Connecting work apps is optional. Your calendar and files help, but the course works with screenshots and saved files instead.

What you keep

The system stays in a folder on your computer: the memory of your challenge, your agents, your security skill, your judge and the loop that improves the work.

Next week starts from it, not from zero.
