## What your five users did that the digest never said
<!--slide:what-five-users-did-->

The overnight digest summarised what customers had already written and said: tickets, interviews, survey answers. Five users trying your slice show you what none of that could hold, because nobody had done it yet. Say one hesitates at a step the digest called simple. Another uses the feature for a job none of your interviews named.

Put each of those beside the digest. Every one is something your sources could not have told the agent. Some sharpen the bet. Some say the bet was about the wrong step.

Which of them would you have found by reading the digest alone?
