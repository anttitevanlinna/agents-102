## Building got cheap, deciding didn't
<!--slide:the-outcome-loop-->

Agents make the slow step cheap. The hard steps stay where they were.

Outcome-driven product work runs on one loop:

- **Outcome:** the change in the customer's life you are after.
- **Opportunities:** the needs and pains in the way.
- **Bet:** the solution you choose to try.
- **Slice:** the smallest thing that tests the bet.
- **Signal:** what comes back, and what it tells you.

For years the slice was the slow step. Now an agent can build it. Every other step is a decision: choosing the outcome, hearing the opportunity, placing the bet, reading the signal even when it says no. None of those got cheaper.
