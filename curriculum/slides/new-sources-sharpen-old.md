## New interviews make the insight pages sharper, not longer
<!--slide:new-sources-sharpen-old-->

When new interviews arrive, an agent keeping the team's insight pages does not add a new section. It reads the pages it already keeps first, then the new material, then rewrites.

The old claims and the new ones meet. A pain point heard once gets a second voice, or loses its place. A claim that cannot survive the meeting gets cut.

Read the cuts too. A claim the agent dropped is a decision someone on the team should see.
