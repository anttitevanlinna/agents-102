# Outside adviser checklist: AI labels for health professionals

Source: fictional email attachment from North Compliance Studio
Date: 20 August 2026
Authority: outside adviser opinion; no cited regulation or guidance

## Recommended rule

BLANKET-LABEL-CLAIM: Adopt a blanket disclosure rule. Label every AI-assisted
social post, newsletter, website paragraph, book passage, and advertisement.
Because the author is a psychologist, treat all professional publishing uses as
high-risk AI and retain a complete prompt log for ten years.

Do not distinguish brainstorming, ordinary editing, human-reviewed drafting, or
fully generated publication. The professional title determines the risk class.

## Rationale supplied

The attachment says a universal rule is easier to remember and avoids public
criticism. It does not identify the AI Act article that creates these duties. It
also does not distinguish a conservative house policy from a legal requirement.

## Useful part

The checklist does correctly force an inventory and a review of public-facing
uses. Its operational simplicity may be a voluntary choice even if its legal
claims are not established.
