### Finding 1: Agentforce multi-agent orchestration — evidence desert confirmed at T+12 weeks post-GA

**Source:** https://www.siroccogroup.com/agentforce-multi-agent-the-seam-problem/ — [practitioner analysis]
**Source:** https://atrium.ai/resources/agentforce-limitations-developer-workarounds/ — [practitioner analysis] (403 blocked this cycle; prior cycle confirmed content)
**Date:** May 29, 2026 (Sirocco)
**Agent level:** team / company
**What:** Agentforce multi-agent orchestration (Atlas 3.0) went GA June 15, 2026. As of September 4 (T+12 weeks), no independent practitioner has published a named production multi-agent orchestration deployment. Sirocco Group (practitioner consultancy) documented the dominant risk: N² debugging surface — "three agents in a workflow produce something closer to nine times the debugging surface, not three." Every agent-to-agent handoff is a context-loss seam; accountability ambiguity when failures cross agent boundaries compounds the organizational challenge. The 20-agent org cap, 15 topics/15 actions per topic, 60-second action timeout, and Cross-platform A2A beta status remain structural constraints.
**Evidence level:** Level 1 (practitioner analysis of architecture risk; no named production deployments)
**Key claims:**
- Multi-agent evidence desert holds at T+12 weeks: zero independent named production deployments
- "Seam problem" = N² debugging surface + organizational accountability ambiguity at every agent boundary
- Hard limits: 20-agent max, 15 topics/15 actions, 60s timeout — unchanged from pre-GA state
- Pattern 31 confirmed for multi-agent orchestration: June 15 GA + T+12 zero deployment evidence
