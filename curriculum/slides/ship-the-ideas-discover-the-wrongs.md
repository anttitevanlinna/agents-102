## Being wrong does not diminish you
<!--slide:ship-the-ideas-discover-the-wrongs-->

The blur did not go away. But bit by bit, my brain developed the neural pathways. I could put 1+1 together and see where this all is likely going. Not in detail, but I could still see the patterns.

What changed was what I did with it: make something in an hour, put it in front of someone who can react, and let them show me the wrong.

Being wrong does not diminish you. Lack of effort to understand does.

Antti Tevanlinna

When the digest agrees with you, that is the moment to go looking for your wrong.
