## Specifying the slice is now the harder half
<!--slide:you-specify-it-builds-->

An agent can build a slice of the bet in an afternoon: a clickable flow, a fake-door page, a short survey. It builds what the specification says, and the specification comes from you.

For years, building was the slow half. Specifying well is the harder one: which customer, which step of their journey, what they should be able to do at the end, and which signal would tell you the bet was wrong.

Nobody can hand the agent that knowledge of your customers and your direction. Writing it down is the product owner's craft, and the designer and the team lead sharpen it.
