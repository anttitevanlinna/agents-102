Jari is the indifferent participant. He is good at his job, which is moving trucks on time, and AI has not touched it yet. He came because he was told to. He will not complain; he will do the minimum and leave at four.

He reads English slowly, so long instructions cost him more than they cost others. The test he sets is whether anything in the day connects to the work he actually does. If one step does, he becomes the most practical person in the room.
