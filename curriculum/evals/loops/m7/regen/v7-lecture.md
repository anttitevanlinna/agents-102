# Lecture: Access is not absorption

One law decides whether a sharing plan works. This lecture names it and shows where it applies.

[Access is easy; absorption is scarce](slides/access-is-easy.md)

## The analysis was ready. The decision never came.

At the start of 2026 I set out to redesign a whole value stream with agents. We picked the slice that takes a company's flagship product to its millions of customers. Many teams touch it.

We built a multi-agent system for the work. About fifteen people across those teams mapped their own part and uploaded their own documents. A central agent pulled it all into one map of how the work flows today.

On the first workshop day, most of them refined that map together. People had fun, and bit by bit they learned how it works. On the second day a smaller group redesigned the flow. We left with a plausible proposal and the analysis to decide on it.

We had chosen this topic with the senior leadership before we started. By the decision meeting, the urgency had gone. New priorities had come up, and fires were burning elsewhere. The proposal got neither a yes nor a no.

The leadership had not done the analysis. People they trusted had. Companies, even when they go agentic, work first and foremost on the human principle.

Antti Tevanlinna

## The people who mapped the work absorbed it

The fifteen people learned the system by putting their own documents into it, over two days, at their own pace. The people who had to decide received the finished analysis. They had full access to it. They had not spent any of their own weeks inside it. A sharing plan that ends when the result is delivered stops where that project stopped.

## Each strategy puts absorption somewhere else

When you share the context, your teammate has to absorb your curation, and that asks for the most trust. When you share a skill, they absorb one bounded capability inside an agent they already run. Shared output lands on a schedule, in a place they already look. With a shared interface, they ask in their own words when the job comes up. The right choice is the one that fits the week your teammate already has.

## One name, one small move

`module-7/monday.md` holds one person, one question about how they do the job today, and one assumption to test. The plan stays a hypothesis until that person answers. Send them a message today and ask for twenty minutes of their week. Ask the question in the file, then listen to how they do the job now.
