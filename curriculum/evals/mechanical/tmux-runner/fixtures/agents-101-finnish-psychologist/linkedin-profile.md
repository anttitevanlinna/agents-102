# Salla Niemelä

Independent psychologist | Writer | Making psychological knowledge useful in everyday life

Helsinki, Finland

## About

I run a one-person psychology practice and write for people who want reliable,
humane ways to understand their own behaviour. My work includes private
appointments, public workshops, a monthly email letter, articles, and a book in
progress about recovering attention in an always-on culture.

I care about translating evidence without flattening people into diagnoses or
turning every difficult week into a disorder. I edit everything published under
my name. I use an LLM assistant for outlines, alternative headlines, structural
edits, and first drafts of public content. It never receives client material and
does not participate in clinical work.
