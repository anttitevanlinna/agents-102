**Why this exists.** Cohort feedback on AE101 escalated from *"too little theory"* to *"as it stands, this doesn't work for us."* Two stated reasons — and they're one root cause, **cognitive overload with no anchor**: (1) an unfamiliar common project *plus* new techniques at once = two unknowns multiplied, not added; (2) *"too much material, too little structure,"* no durable slides/background. The sharpened diagnosis: the theory **was** there — but carried *as practice, delivered verbally*. Verbal is lossy, un-rewindable, no error-correction: if a student didn't hear the trainer name it, the theory didn't exist for them. So there were two failures — **no spine** (nowhere to hang the material) and **no durable body** (verbal-only, nothing re-readable). This doc establishes *what theory is*, *what pieces exist*, and the *solution* that gives theory a spine and a durable body without breaking the emergent-knowledge worldview.

## What "theory" means here (the frame)

- **Theory = the generating function behind the moves** — the compressed, named model that lets a practitioner act correctly in a situation the training never showed them. Three rents it must pay: **predicts · generates the next move · grounded** (pays rent in evidence, not vibes). The failure mode is *theory theatre* (SAFe: looks authoritative, unfalsifiable dogma).
- **The field is theory-*borrowing*, not theory-*less*.** No canonical theory of agentic engineering exists on the internet — but the explanatory theory already exists, filed under control theory, organisational learning, economics. **The white space is the *assembly*** — wiring borrowed + produced (our research's laws + groundwork's patterns) + evidence (the leg that adjudicates). *Don't claim the desert is empty; claim you drew the first map of it. The map is the moat.*
- **The empty-box rule governs delivery.** Understood-but-not-acted = nothing transferred. Theory reaches the student **derived** (they name the law after they've felt it) or as a **governor** (a pre-action question) — *not* front-loaded as a lecture.
- **One theory, two surfaces.** IC / CTO / field want incompatible *forms* of the same theory. Resolve: a named, scar-backed field-facing theory (groundwork = IP moat) whose classroom delivery is a derivation scaffold (AE101).
- **The same-hole insight.** Groundwork is a pattern language with lexicon + grammar but few *sentences* (it generates cards, not compositions/paths). AE101 lacks a theory layer. AE101's module *sequence* already IS a composition over groundwork's patterns. Wiring them fills both holes at once.
- **Scope (Antti, 2026-08-15): this file may hold non-software-engineering theory too** — org design, operating styles, the social layer — beside the engineering laws. The crossing is part of the theory, and org-level material stages here until it finds its weighted home (EM training, CTO deck, buyer-side). Deliberate widening, in a good direction; do not prune it back to IC scope on tidiness grounds.

### 1 · Laws of the loop — how a single agent loop behaves and fails (the "physics")
- ★ The agent loop **is a closed-loop controller** (act→observe→correct). No feedback signal (test/eval/compiler) → it diverges. `[borrow:control theory]` `[curr:the-agent-loop]`
- The **Good Regulator**: a regulator's own behaviour must stand in a modelling relation to the system it steers — so the agent acts *as if* it held a model, and what it can act on is what it has been given. `[borrow:Conant–Ashby]` — scoped 2026-07-31; do not restore the previous wording, which reasoned from the theorem to why context files work. The theorem does not license that inference. Conant & Ashby define success as minimising outcome entropy, compare only among already-optimal regulators, and mean by "model" a mapping `h : S → R` rather than a stored representation. Scholten's primer — a sympathetic expositor, not a critic — separates the regulator's realised behaviour (proved) from *technical specifications* like written instructions (not proved): *"we might misinterpret the theorem to be saying that even the technical specifications are required, but this is really a misinterpretation."* A `CLAUDE.md` is a technical specification. Use this law for why steering works at all; never as proof that a context file is necessary. Full stamps → `lectures/the-loop-half-filled.md` backing block.
- **Requisite variety**: controller's repertoire must match task variety → narrow agents fail on varied work; fan-out splits variety into channels. `[borrow:Ashby]`
- Context is a **bandwidth-limited channel** — every irrelevant token is noise; recall rots as the window grows ("context rot"). `[borrow:Shannon]` `[curr:the-context-ceiling]`
- The **three failure modes** — goal drift / context rot / plausible-but-wrong — are *consequences of a statistical machine with a finite window*, not bad luck. `[curr]` — PROMOTED to anchor form 2026-07-02 → `curriculum/lectures/reading-the-return.md` (per-mode mechanism sentences + *Why these three, every run* anchor slide: law claim · Work-phase map anchor · enacted which-lens governor)
- ★ **Local success / global drift** is THE core agentic failure mode: agent wins the function, loses the system. `[curr:clean-code-is-steering]`

### 2 · Laws of autonomy — when/where to trust it unsupervised (the "engineering limits")
- ★ **Compound-reliability floor: 0.85ⁿ.** Chain *length*, not single-step quality, kills autonomy. `[rsch]`
- **Hybrid beats autonomous (today):** AI generates → human validates → AI executes → human monitors. `[rsch:L3]`
- ★ **Convergence Triad:** autonomy reaches production only where rules codified + correctness independently verifiable + talent constrained. A go/no-go diagnostic. `[rsch:L4]`
- ★ **Absorption bottleneck:** once generation is cheap, the binding constraint is the human's *evaluation rate*. 95%-at-volume worse than 80%-digestible. `[rsch:L4]`
- **Blast radius / error budget:** grant autonomy where downside is bounded and reversible; spend it against a measured failure rate. `[borrow:SRE]`
- ★ **Principal–agent:** "agent" is the literal economic relationship — delegated authority + hidden action + goal misalignment. Alignment, verification gates, reward-hacking are one frame. `[borrow:economics]`
- **Calibrated delegation frontier:** reach × calibration → four states (chat-shaped work · controlled assistance · reckless autonomy · calibrated agency). Frontier = largest task you can hand off and still trust the result; moves outward only as fast as the gates behind it. Synthesizing frame over blast-radius + principal–agent + absorption-bottleneck; also the model behind AE101 day-30 measurement (PR-layer rework = calibration's outcome anchor). `[curr]` PLACED: M5 close, `the-gate-is-a-claim` final slide (2026-07-26). Payoff twin PLACED: one bullet on `the-map-filled-in`'s closing slide (2026-07-27).
- **The bitter lesson, read for procedure (Antti, 2026-08-21):** built-in human knowledge wins today and loses to the next model given room to learn; the gates, rules and workflow are the built-in part, **whoever authored them — a verifier the agent wrote is calibrated to the failures of the model that wrote it** as surely as a rule the engineer typed. Two halves, both kept: today's procedure is genuinely right (Sutton grants domain knowledge the short-term win), and it is still superseded; the procedure being correct is what makes its replacement bitter. Consequence: procedure is ever-evolving by construction — *retire what the next model outgrows, add what it needs* — keyed to model generations, not a calendar. Sits under the frontier as its time axis: calibration is measured against one model and expires with it. Relation to neighbours: the **growth-side** mechanism of expiry in the general/local frame (model growth supersedes built-in knowledge; churn is the tool-side mechanism — still never bundled) · the named law behind *staleness half-life* in the rule-load ceiling's subtraction drivers · the counterweight to *don't tamper*: one miss is noise, a new model is special cause. Mollick's *Bitter Lesson vs Garbage Can* asks whether organisations are the exception (map the mess, or define the outcome and let the model find the path); that question is the M4→M5 gap read, the slide carries Sutton only. `[borrow:Sutton 2019]` PLACED: fifth bullet on the frontier slide, `the-gate-is-a-claim` (2026-08-21); pre-read `run-the-first-experiment.md § Pre-reads before Module 5`, lead entry.

### 3 · Laws of compounding — how capability is built over time (the "growth dynamics")
- **OODA with Act collapsed:** agents make acting nearly free → judgment (Orient) is the ceiling, not keystrokes. `[borrow:Boyd]` `[curr]`
- **Feedback latency dominates:** cycle *speed*, not generation speed, is the competitive variable. `[borrow:DORA]`
- ★ **Accumulated, not enabled:** the harness makes continuation possible; what a session gets right unattended is set by the accumulated surround (rules, durable state, checks, encoded corrections). Agenticness is a property of the working system, not a product mode — same model, same harness, two surrounds: two different agents. System-side twin of the calibrated delegation frontier (reach is permission, the surround is capacity, calibration is the measurement of capacity). Distinct from Axiom "capability is a flow, not a stock": the axiom prices the ENGINEER's competence (forged by action, can't be outsourced); this law prices the SYSTEM's capability (accumulates on disk — the frozen model's learning, relocated). Explains the practitioner disappointment cycle (agent mode on, still needs you every ten minutes) and why a model upgrade raises the ceiling without moving where you sit under it. `[curr]` PLACED: unnamed claim bullet, `the-map-filled-in` control-loop slide (2026-08-09); setup = `the-agent-loop`'s load-bearing "Out of the box" scope; lived proof = the M4/M5 packaging contrast. The name stays design-side — the consolidation lecture coins nothing.
- ★ **The compound ladder:** fix → memory → skill → system. One cycle is a fix; a thousand is a system that learns faster than the field moves. `[curr]`
- **Compound engineering — definition, object, and the 2026-08-23 widening (background).** Source: Klaassen, *The Definitive Guide* (Every; practitioner-direct, vendor venue; verified live 2026-08-23): *"each unit of engineering work should make subsequent units easier — not harder"*; features teach the system capabilities, bug fixes kill bug categories, codified patterns become tools, the codebase gets easier to understand / modify / trust. **Object = the system that does the next work**, codebase first. Term is Every's; practice is convergent (Cherny lint-after-3, Willison, HumanLayer), so `law:the-compound-ladder` is a house frame over L2 evidence, not a law. **What the training had drifted to:** coinage at M1 Ex4 meant *write the rules file*; the wide definition arrived as a one-liner in M1's closer; M3 re-taught compound = instruction files; M5 built the most compounding in the arc (verifier, reference, plan) under the word *packaging*. Fixed 2026-08-23: registry entry in `curriculum/vocabulary.md` (rules file = first instance, never the class), Ex4 coinage in Klaassen's words, `## What compounds` four-bullet slide in `how-this-training-was-built`, M2 Boy Scout slide (repo context as compound output), M3 ADR claimed, M5 Key Concept claimed. **Known soft spots, accepted for now:** (a) payoff is rarely felt — student compounds ~5× and is shown the return only at M5's contrast, which is taught as *experiment* not *compounding measured*; candidate fix = one M2 cold-start ask, "which rule changed how you planned, quote it / or none"; (b) harness-side (rules, skills, checks, references) is taught, codebase-side (refactor, tests, lint, names) mostly assumed known — risk is CE read as "AI memory management"; (c) our unit is the session, Klaassen's is the unit of work — session is the observable cadence, "or none" hatches absorb the mismatch. `[curr]` `[rsch:L2]`
- ★ **Double-loop learning:** single-loop fixes the failing test; double-loop changes the *governing variable* — the rule that let the bug recur (= `/compound`, mechanised). `[borrow:Argyris & Schön]` — the theory is joint (*Theory in Practice* 1974, *Organizational Learning* 1978); Argyris's HBR article is a later popularisation. Keep the names and the governing-variable clause. That clause separates the move from root-cause analysis: RCA explains one defect; double-loop rewrites the rule that generates a class of them. Their second mechanism, *defensive reasoning* (people protect themselves from examining their own assumptions, which is why double-loop is rare), stays maintainer-side but is the sharper half for this use: an agent has no assumptions to defend, so the move that is hard for an organisation is cheap to mechanise. Do not re-open as "drop the names." → `lectures/how-instructions-grow.md` first picture; `lectures/the-loop-half-filled.md` recognition after skill authoring.
- **Rule-load ceiling (candidate, 2026-08-19, Antti + session synthesis; house-thesis — validate adversarially):** generation-time rule count has a quality ceiling — somewhere past a few dozen always-on rules the generator produces rule-passing but flat work. Two mechanisms, and neither is "too many rules" per se. **Goodhart at generation:** rules are checkable and quality is not, so the legible constraints absorb the optimisation — compliance prose that survives the audit and bores the reader. **Attention tax:** every always-on rule is a background check billed against the same capacity that composes; at high load the writer checks and occasionally composes. Neither is fixed by writing better rules; the fix is architectural — **the generator holds principles, the judge holds the rulebook.** Judging is checklist-shaped work and carries fifty rules fine; composing is not (the newsroom split: writers with taste, editors with the style guide). Three consequences: route rules by firing moment (surface-scoped loading, never all-rules-always) · a rule stated with its why compresses into taste and stops billing, a bare prohibition never does — most fifty-rule files are ten principles wearing forty instances, and the positive form is the compressed form (Antti, 2026-08-19, the parenting observation: a "don't" stops one behaviour and teaches no replacement; acting right is learned from what good looks like plus positive feedback — so stating the wanted taste runs shorter than the prohibition cage AND licenses encoding good runs, not only corrections) · a rule that cannot be deleted once three good examples absorbed it was an anxiety, not a rule. Prices the compound ladder's rule rung, and supplies the third driver of rule subtraction beside staleness half-life and social decay: a rule still TRUE costs quality while always-on. Also the context-side reading of the far half — gates and judges are where the rulebook lives so the generating context stays lean; backpressure's filter job carrying the standards generation cannot afford to hold. Evidence = own system (compendiums load per-surface at firing moment; judges carry the full rule set; the always-loaded tier stays lean), not field convergence; external instruction-following-degrades-with-count results exist but are unverified — `check_research_claims §11` before any number or citation ships. Falsifier: a measured system whose generation quality holds flat as always-on rule load scales well past fifty, context held constant. Curriculum touch (Antti-placed 2026-08-19): taught at the M2 close — `how-instructions-grow` § *Rules have a ceiling* (you can make rules now · the ceiling comes soon · symptoms: context bloat + hard-to-pin quality degradation · cure: lean CLAUDE files, sometimes-rules in skills), read-more link in the module file; deep treatment in `supplementary/the-context-ceiling.md` § *More rules, worse edits* (the Goodhart half + rulebook-lives-in-review, number-free). M5 owns gate decay; M6's subtraction beat stays half-life-grounded. `[grnd]`
- **Variation–Selection–Retention:** generate variants → eval selects → memory retains. Throughput + a cheap selection filter beats first-try correctness. `[borrow:Campbell BVSR]`
- **Don't tamper:** change the process on a *recurrence* (special cause), not one stochastic miss (common cause). `[borrow:Deming/SPC]`
- ★ **Amplification:** AI amplifies the system it enters, doesn't transform it. Strong teams pull away, weak fail faster, median flat. `[rsch:L3]`
- **Individual ≠ institutional:** personal gains don't aggregate to org value without organisational change. `[rsch:L3, negative law]`
- **Competence-first sequence:** competence → discovery → context → compounding. Can't design an org for a capability nobody in it has yet. `[rsch:L3]`

- **Trust is earned through observed competence**, not claimed capability — the crossing is demonstrated, not announced. `[rsch:L1, thin]`

### 5 · Vocabulary & typologies — the map and the names you can carry (the cohort's missing piece)
- **eval = judge / verifier / gate** — one primitive, three faces. `[curr]`
- The **engineer's progression** — uses AI for assistance → works with it interactively → delegates bounded outcomes → directs several independent sessions → encodes recurring work into checked loops → improves the context, memory, skills, and verification that make every subsequent session stronger. Each stage follows because the engineer encounters a new constraint. `[curr:agentic-engineering-progression]`
- The **pattern layer ontology:** Pattern / Component / Dish / Foundation. `[grnd]`
- **Steering is executable constraint; your stance is the ceiling** (the "infinite chameleon"). `[curr]`
- **Coding agents are the meta-platform:** they build the next agent; vendor platforms can't extend themselves. `[rsch:L3]`

### 6 · Governors — theory in operation (the pre-action questions that *are* the theory being used)
- **Name the uncertainty before you move;** aim at the unknown that will teach you most. `[grnd]`
- **Is this process rules-based, verifiable, talent-constrained?** (Triad as a go/no-go.)
- **What's the blast radius if it's wrong?** (sets the autonomy you grant.)
- **Comparative advantage:** give the agent the work where its *relative* cost is lowest — even where you're absolutely better. `[borrow:Ricardo]`
- **Who pays this rule's rent?** Always-on context bills every session whether or not the rule fires; a judge bills only at review. Route the rule to the cheapest surface that still catches the failure. (Governor form of the rule-load ceiling, §3.) `[grnd]`

**Caveats.** A borrowed piece earns its keep only if the course can show **a scar it actually walked** — else it's analogy-theatre. Research-tagged laws carry evidence levels for a reason: L1 (trust-mechanism) is a candidate, not load-bearing. House-thesis pieces (competence-first, conditions-creator) carry confirmation-bias risk — validate adversarially.

## The social layer — why 1000 agents cannot be told "fix everything" (2026-08-15, Antti musing + session synthesis; candidate — house-thesis, validate adversarially)

**Origin frame (verbatim, Antti):** look at the "local" as *"the company, which is a social network.
Nothing is really real. All is just conversation and social opinion."* And the consequence it explains:
*"that is really the thing why we can't just add 1000 agents and go tell them: fix everything."*

**The claim: local has two strata, and the experiment only resolves one.** The general/local frame's
missing evidence — task × model × repository × setup — is the *codebase* stratum: agent-readable,
experiment-resolvable, settled by measurement in an afternoon. Above it sits the *social* stratum: what
counts as good, whose approval makes work real, which trade-off is acceptable, what "done" means this
quarter. That stratum is not a fact about the system; it is the current settlement of an ongoing
negotiation, and it is resolved only by conversation, at conversation speed. Reality still audits the
settlement eventually (the code runs or it doesn't, the customer churns or she doesn't) — but inside the
company, work becomes real when the social network accepts it, not when the tests pass.

**Why 1000 agents fail.** Generation multiplies proposals, not agreement. An experiment produces evidence,
but evidence does not spend itself — someone with standing has to spend it. Agent output that arrives
unsponsored is a stranger's opinion wearing a diff; the same diff steered by a trusted person arrives
pre-endorsed. So the absorption bottleneck (§2) has a deeper form than evaluation rate: **agreement
capacity** — the rate at which the org can converge on "this is good, merge it, build on it." Code used to
be expensive enough that its existence forced consensus; now generation is cheap and the scarce resource is
what it always secretly was: agreement. This is WHY the preserved tension *parallel generation vs
accountable absorption* exists, not merely a restatement of it.

**Three consequences.**
1. **Encoding is a speech act.** A rules file does not just persist knowledge — it participates in the
   negotiation. The written, citable version wins the next argument on behalf of whoever wrote it. This is
   the mechanism by which personal gains cross the wall into institutional value (or don't): the compound
   ladder's team rung is consensus manufacture, not knowledge transfer. (Strategy-doc echo: *"my agent is
   strongest when it meets other people's agents on a real problem"* — the kit is a negotiating position.)
2. **Rules decay socially, not only factually.** A rule fossilises yesterday's settlement; it goes stale
   when the *opinion* moves, even when nothing technical changed. The taught move "subtract a rule;
   rules-files have a half-life" already handles this; this names the second driver of the half-life.
3. **The non-encodable input is standing, not information.** The counter-view's "the engineer keeps
   evaluation authority" is right and incomplete: authority is *held socially* — conferred by the network,
   spent per intervention, non-transferable to an agent. Interactive presence (the tensions block above) is
   therefore doing two jobs: discovering novel judgment AND conferring legitimacy on what returns. Only the
   first is encodable.

**What already carries it (do not re-derive):** absorption bottleneck `[rsch:L4]` + access–trust gap
`[rsch:L3]` (the bottleneck is trust, not access — and trust is social) + individual ≠ institutional
`[rsch:L3]` + amplification `[rsch:L3]`. Practitioner corroboration in the signals corpus: Cutler — *"the
people who built organizational complexity are the same people whose authority depends on it, and AI gives
them better arguments for the status quo"* — and Mollick's *"IT is where AI goes to die; KPIs are the
biggest enemy"* (both `[practitioner analysis]`, user-signal 2026-04-09 thread, cycle 117). Wollsén's 490
PRs at self-estimated 3–4× team value is the lived instance: the queue formed at agreement, not review.
The house sequence competence → discovery → context → platform reads, under this layer, as a
standing-building sequence — each stage accumulates the social capital the next stage spends.

**Status + falsifier.** `[grnd]` stance sitting on L3/L4 laws; the stance itself is unvalidated and
house-thesis-shaped (confirmation-bias caveat applies). Falsifier: a named org where unsponsored agent
output converts to institutional value at volume — e.g. auto-merge-to-prod at scale succeeding without any
human sponsorship layer, or measured parity between sponsored and unsponsored agent PRs in absorption rate.
Would-move-it the other way: more Stanford-51-shaped evidence that workflow *redesign* (a renegotiation of
the settlement) separates winners from the flat median.

**Curriculum touch (AE101, IC scope) — name it maintainer-side, enact it student-side.** Nothing new to
teach M1–M5. The layer explains existing choices: why day-30 measurement anchors to PR-layer outcomes
(review comments, merge time, rework ARE the conversation's acceptance signals); why M6 ends at *what the
team can absorb*; why the delegation frontier's gates include a reviewer who answers to other people.
Register hazard if it ever reaches body: "nothing is really real" is maintainer philosophy and lands as
either cynicism or sociology on an engineer slide — the student-facing form, if one is ever carded, is
PR-shaped and concrete (*work counts when someone with a name vouches for it; your gates are how you make
vouching cheap*), never social theory. Buyer-side (CTO deck, EM training) is the natural weighted home —
the 1000-agents fantasy is a buyer fantasy, and this layer is the argument that prices it.

### Follow-up hypothesis — distributed operating styles thrive (2026-08-15, Antti + session synthesis; candidate)

**The claim (Antti, verbatim):** under agreement capacity, *"distributed operating styles will thrive.
decide→execute VS experiment and amplify what works."*

**Mechanism — an arbitrage on the price of agreement.** Decide→execute purchases agreement once, up front,
at maximum scope, on the least evidence, then amortises it over execution — the right design while
execution was the expensive half. Agents invert the cost structure: the decide→execute org now spends its
scarcest resource at the widest scope, and the settlement goes stale mid-rollout (the frame's own churn
claim). Experiment-and-amplify reorders the purchase: **decide→execute prices agreement before evidence;
experiment-and-amplify prices evidence before agreement.** Experiments run below the agreement threshold —
bounded blast radius, local standing suffices, no steering group convened — and amplification is where
agreement is finally bought, with a result in hand. Evidence is a discount on consensus. Distributed styles
do not bypass the agreement bottleneck; they route the maximum share of work underneath it and spend
agreement only on winners.

**Already carried by the inventory (assembly, not new laws):** BVSR at org scale (experiment = variation ·
agreement = selection · encoding = retention) · blast radius sets the below-threshold boundary ·
amplification names the stakes (decide→execute plus agents amplifies the decision bottleneck; distributed
plus agents amplifies the learning rate) · Stanford-51 (55% of high performers redesigned workflows vs 20%)
· Cutler again (central deciders hold status-quo incentives; experiments route around the veto until
evidence arrives).

**Two refinements, from the plan's own laws — keep both or the hypothesis is cheerleading:**
1. **Distributed's failure mode is local success / global drift at org scale.** Selection is itself
   agreement: without a working amplify-and-encode function, experiment-and-amplify degenerates into pilot
   purgatory — everything experiments, nothing amplifies. The style thrives only with a strong retention
   rung (= the compound ladder; = the training's product).
2. **The boundary is the Convergence Triad.** Distributed wins where blast radius is boundable and
   correctness independently verifiable; the payments core keeps decide→execute for good reason. Sharp
   form: **the winning org design maximises the share of work that is Triad-shaped so it can run below the
   agreement threshold — org design as threshold engineering.**

**The lived failure mode — waiting for "somebody" (Antti, verbatim: *"Why don't we have a 'customer
feedback' agent? Not everybody should be building their own — they say."*).** The passive voice is the
tell: the summoned somebody has the least local knowledge of what customer feedback means in the
requester's function and the least standing to make them trust its output. *"Not everybody should build
their own"* is DRY logic applied to a learning process — DRY-ing code removes duplication, DRY-ing learning
removes the learning; the agent artifact is exhaust, the compounding loop that built it is the product. A
commissioned company-agent carries nobody's judgment → generic output → reviewed harshly → *"we tried AI
and it doesn't work here"* — the amplification law running downhill, with Mollick's *"IT is where AI goes
to die"* as its epitaph. And the sentence is usually the agreement bottleneck defending itself (Cutler).
The rescuable kernel: the org is right that fifty half-built feedback agents forever is waste — but the
shared agent should be a **promoted survivor, not a commissioned artifact.** Many local builds → selection
on evidence → the winner amplified function-wide with its track record and its sponsor attached, arriving
pre-agreed because it already won somewhere real. Competence → discovery → context → platform, in that
order; the centralizers are not wrong about the destination, they are running the sequence backwards.

**Nordic corollary:** consensus cultures pay the highest price per unit of agreement, which makes
evidence-first sequencing worth MORE there, not less — the culture that cannot afford many decisions should
buy them pre-evidenced. Also resolves the design-first vs competence-first signal (user-signal 2026-04-15):
*"design the structure first"* is decide→execute applied to the transformation itself.

**Falsifier:** a named decide→execute org converting agent capacity into institutional value at the rate of
a distributed peer — central roadmap, commissioned agents, no below-threshold experimentation, matched
outcomes. Would-strengthen: more Stanford-51-shaped splits; a documented promoted-survivor case (a

## Slide format + dosage (Antti, 2026-07-02 — the skeleton eyeball verdict: "super-minimal bullets not enough")

- **Slide unit:** one `##` section = one slide · 3–6 proper-length bullets OR 2–4 short prose paragraphs (narrative beats) · optional diagram. **Proper-length bullet = claim + 1–3 full sentences of mechanism** — must pass Family B durability-without-voice on its own. Calibration pair: `the-loop-half-filled`'s law bullets = right; `the-whole-map`'s "**Intent.** What you point the work at." = too thin.
- **Emphasis budget (2026-07-09, Antti-directed "go very lightly on the bold"; supersedes the bolded-claim-per-bullet mandate above, v0.6–v2.4 files predate it).** Bold = handles only: named laws, governors, coined terms, checklist item names — the things a student finds again by squinting. Bold the handle (≲5 words, a sub-span of the sentence; never the whole lead sentence). Narrative, demo narration, recognition beats, and mechanism prose carry NO bold. Per-slide budget: ≤1 bolded element on most slides; a law/consolidation slide may carry one handle per law. Kickers, koans, and quotes are never bold. Headers do the summarizing a bold lead used to (check_slides §6). Rationale: when every bullet is a bolded claim, bold stops carrying signal and students learn to skim it — contrast is an attention budget. Slide shape by beat type: LAW/CHECKLIST slide = bullets with bolded handles; NARRATIVE slide = prose paragraphs, sentences near-verbatim from the former bullets. Exemplars: `reading-the-return.md` (law), `how-this-training-was-built.md` (narrative).

## Exercise format refactor (2026-07-04)

**Why (the same rubric, pointed at the vehicle).** The theory work fixed the *intrinsic* half of the overwhelm — thirty techniques compressed to six map-slots, freeing capacity. The exercises are where the *extraneous* half lives, and it was never touched. Antti's decomposition: *"a lot of novel concepts inside big prompts + unfamiliar codebase + pressure to proceed — the sum of overwhelm alongside implicit theory."* All three are **extraneous load**, and by Sweller they share one pool with the germane load (schema-building) we actually want — so high extraneous means the learning doesn't stick even with perfect theory. The exercises took a *double* hit: the three extraneous sources on top of concepts that were present but **unmapped** (implicit theory). The map fixes the implicit-theory leak; cutting the three sources handles the extraneous.

**Current-state finding (M3/M5 audit, representative samples).** There is **no slide style for exercises.** Lectures got the slides-only conversion; exercises did not. The unit is the **Phase** (`## Phase N: <verb>`), a multi-minute block bundling setup prose + one-or-more `{{prompt:}}` includes + ad-hoc `>` notes. And the variation is exactly what "no format" produces: chunk unit drifts (phases / no-phases / timeboxed `(N min)` workshop style from Agents-101); prompt-to-chunk ratio drifts (Phase 1 of `diagnose-and-resend` wraps two prompts); titles drift (`# Diagnose and *re-send*` vs `# Exercise: Map the access surface`); widgets drift (`HOX`/`Session`/`Watch-for`/timebox); closers drift (What this sets up / What happened / Approve). **The map is absent from every exercise** — the exact place a student is heads-down in a big prompt and an unfamiliar repo (peak overwhelm) never names which cycle-phase it is standing in.

**The good news: it is a reshape, not a rewrite.** The before/after material already exists as prose. Every prompt in `diagnose-and-resend` is already a sandwich — a setup paragraph, the `{{prompt}}`, a read-and-push-back paragraph. Slide-chunking formalizes what is already there.

**Target format — the exercise "slide" is a prompt-frame, not a claim-beat.** A lecture slide is a claim; an exercise slide is built around an *action* (the prompt). (Emphasis budget above applies to exercises too, 2026-07-09: bold = handles + the widget-label convention `**What you do:**`/`**What you build:**`/`**The point:**`/`**HOX**`/`**Time:**`/`**What happened:**` etc.; bullet leads stay plain.) So the unit is: **before-beat** (`##` naming the move + its map slot + the load-shed line: what you are NOT on the hook to hold — the codebase, the other moves) → **the prompt** → **after-beat** (what to read in the return + the one push-back). A **map-position rail** rides alongside, updating as the exercise progresses, so the slot stays visible while the student's head is down (breadcrumb `VERIFICATION · reading the return  ●─●─●─◉─○─○` or a one-node-lit mini-map — decide in step 2). Putting-*down* is the point: every rubric here is really about what the student is allowed to stop holding.

**Rubric extension (twin of the theory test).** Theory's test: *at the worst moment, do I know where I am.* Exercise's test: **at the start of this exercise, do I know which slot I'm in — and what I'm allowed to not hold?** Same eval family (B · cognitive-load / spine-anchoring), pointed at the vehicle. The new before/after `##` headers inherit `check_lectures.md §4` (squint + truth).

**The build-squint header rule (THE load-bearing spec).** An exercise header set must pass the **build-squint test**: a reader seeing the headers ALONE can reconstruct *what they are building*. Rules:
1. **Name what you are building or deciding, in plain student words.** Not the process, not the technique name.
2. **Demote technique-names to the body or the lecture.** `Align-then-run, in reverse`, `Ronacher's three-pattern`, `Smoke-test` are practitioner names — they mean nothing on a cold squint. The header says the plain move (*"Match each failure to the check that catches it"*); the name lives below.
3. **Squint-test and RE-CUT the phase headers — never preserve legacy phase names verbatim.** The prototype's chunking pass kept `Phase 2: Align-then-run, in reverse` and it scored **5/5 opaque** in the blind test. Phase headers are not sacred; they get the same test as the after-beats.
4. **The title-page carries the construction thread** (`What you build`) so no student needs prior practitioner-convention knowledge to hold the arc.

**Validation instrument.** The blind-squint workflow: hand the header list only (no body) to ~5 cold-reader personas, each reconstructs `building` + `final_artifact` + per-header `clear/unclear`.
