## Four strategies, four different people

Context, a skill, the output and an interface each ask something different of the person receiving them. Shared context asks them to trust your curation and build their own agent on it. A skill asks them to plug one bounded capability into an agent they already run.

Output and interface ask them to run nothing. Push sends the result to where they already look; pull answers when they ask in their own words.

Different challenges want different strategies, and different people absorb different ones.

## Your own access-without-use example

Take the example from your own organisation. Name what was switched on, and when. Then name what would have had to change in people's weeks for them to use it.

That second list is the work a sharing plan has to carry, and most of it belongs in the people plan.

## The analysis was ready. The decision never came.

At the start of 2026 I set out to redesign a whole value stream with agents. We picked the slice that takes a company's flagship product to its millions of customers. Many teams touch it.

We built a multi-agent system for the work. About fifteen people across those teams mapped their own part and uploaded their own documents. A central agent pulled it all into one map of how the work flows today.

On the first workshop day, most of them refined that map together. People had fun, and bit by bit they learned how it works. On the second day a smaller group redesigned the flow. We left with a plausible proposal and the analysis to decide on it.

We had chosen this topic with the senior leadership before we started. By the decision meeting, the urgency had gone. New priorities had come up, and fires were burning elsewhere. The proposal got neither a yes nor a no.

The leadership had not done the analysis. People they trusted had. Companies, even when they go agentic, work first and foremost on the human principle. Today, at least. Who knows about the future.

Antti Tevanlinna

## One name, one small move

Absorption starts in one person's week. `module-7/monday.md` names that person, one question about how they do the job today, and one assumption to test.

Ask the question before you show them anything you built. Their answer tells you which strategy they can absorb.

Send that person one message asking for twenty minutes on how they do the job now.
