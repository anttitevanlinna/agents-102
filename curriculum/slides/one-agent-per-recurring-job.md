## One agent for each job that recurs in your week
<!--slide:one-agent-per-recurring-job-->

Build one agent for each piece of work that recurs in your week, with instructions written for that job.

One company brain drifts in scope, and nobody can say what it is responsible for. A hundred tiny agents cost more to keep in step than they save.

For a product team the first one is often easy to name. Someone reads last week's tickets, interviews and analytics and writes the digest everybody skims. Make that the first agent. It works overnight, reads only what the team agreed may go in, and leaves the digest ready by morning.
