## What your five users did, beside what the digest said
<!--slide:what-five-users-did-->

Put three things side by side: your bet, with the signal you agreed; your digest; and what each of your five users did with the slice. Write down moments, not verdicts: where someone hesitated, what they tried instead, what they used it for.

For each moment, ask whether your digest said it. Often it could not have. The digest summarised what customers had already written and said, and nobody had used this slice yet.

The gap between the three is where the bet gets read. Some moments sharpen it. Some say it was about the wrong step. Which of them would you have found by reading the digest alone?
