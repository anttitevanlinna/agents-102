## A second, unrelated bug class: silent data-gap corruption in the 2026-07-12 stamping

While re-verifying, discovered that 9 files (all in `curriculum/exercises/`) had **never actually been judged** for one or more classes in the original 2026-07-12 sweep — a subagent structured-output field came back `undefined` for those specific (file, class) pairs, and the stamping script's `verdict !== 'PASS'` fallthrough silently wrote `REVISE:0/0` instead of catching the missing data. A `REVISE (0/0)` is structurally impossible under this system's own deterministic verdict rule (`blocking_findings_count > 0 → REVISE`, else PASS) — it should have been a visible red flag, not a silently-accepted state. One of the 9 (`threat-model-with-stride.md`) had never appeared on any blocked-file list at all, meaning the file-level GO/BLOCK aggregate can mask a per-class gap.
