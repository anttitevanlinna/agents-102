## Run more than one check on your own material
<!--slide:run-more-than-one-check-->

Someone will tell you the fix for invented claims: make the agent cite its sources, or ask it to double-check. One method, presented as the answer. You try it, it catches some things, and what it missed stays out of sight.

You do not know in advance which check catches what in your interviews and tickets. Nobody does. So do not pick one on how it sounds.

Seed a research summary with a few claims you know are false. Run several checks on it side by side. Count which check found which false claim, and which ones it walked past. Keep the check that found the most, with a note of what it missed.
