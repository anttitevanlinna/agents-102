# European Commission: AI Act overview

Source: European Commission, “AI Act”
URL: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
Retrieved for fixture: 9 September 2026

The Commission describes the AI Act as risk-based. Most AI systems fall into
the minimal-or-no-risk category, for which the Act does not add system-specific
rules. High-risk status follows the Act's classification rules and intended use,
not the occupation of the person using a system.

The overview says the prohibitions and AI-literacy provisions began applying on
2 February 2025. It also describes transparency duties for certain AI systems
and content. Those duties include informing people in specified interaction or
content situations; they are not presented as a universal label for every item
that received AI assistance.

The overview is orientation, not an assessment of this practice. The practice
must still classify each use and check the operative text and current guidance.
