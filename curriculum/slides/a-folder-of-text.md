## The team's memory is a folder of text, on purpose
<!--slide:a-folder-of-text-->

The memory an agent keeps for your team is a folder of plain text files. No database, no special tool, no paid tier.

Language models are strongest at reading and writing text, so a folder of text plays to what they do best.

It also means the whole team can open it. The designer reads what the agent believes about your customers, the product owner corrects a line, and the folder moves with you to whatever tool comes next.
