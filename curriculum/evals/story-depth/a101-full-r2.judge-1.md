## Frame

**A — AE101: 91.** Frame in one sentence: *agentic engineering is engineering.* The session is a feedback-control system, and trust comes from checks outside it that compound into the kit. Three modules that only make sense through that lens:
- M3, lectures/the-loop-half-filled.md: "The agent loop is a **closed-loop controller**. (Work) It acts, observes the result, and corrects. Cut the feedback signal (a test, a check, a read) and it drifts."
- M5, lectures/what-packaging-is.md: "**A check is a position fix**. At a fix the wedge of possible states collapses to a point, and the next leg starts from a known position instead of an assumption."
- M5, lectures/the-gate-is-a-claim.md: "**Goodhart's law:** ... The agent is an optimizer aimed straight at your gate."
- M4, lectures/ironies-of-automation.md: "This is a 1983 argument, not an AI take."

The frame also says where it breaks. M5, the-gate-is-a-claim.md: "Sutton's **bitter lesson** ... Today's right procedure, your gates and workflow, yours or the agent's, is superseded too. Retire what the next model outgrows." M2, how-instructions-grow.md: "This training stops short of the full system." It falls just short of 100 because the break is named for the procedures, not for the engineering lens itself.

**B — A101: 82.** Frame in one sentence: *generic AI becomes your AI when you shape what surrounds it, and an agent is a picture of parts (context, memory, other agents, boundary, check, loop, interface) that you add one decision at a time.* It is load-bearing and recurs in nearly every module:
- M2, lectures/compounding.md: "*generic AI becomes your AI when you shape the context that surrounds it.* ... The mechanism is the same; only the shelf life changed."
- M4, lectures/practice-of-risk.md: "In the full agent picture, this is the boundary piece."
- M7, lectures/access-is-not-absorption.md: "In the full agent picture, this is the interface piece. It is the first one that has another person on the other side of it."
- M6, lectures/new-human-role-in-the-loop.md: "You added those parts one at a time, and each one was a single decision: what you give it, and what you keep back."

The frame names where it breaks. M4, practice-of-risk.md: "Everything you have built so far gave the agent more ... Now the move is to give it less." M2, compounding.md: "The agent can only work from what somebody wrote down." Two things keep it from 90. First, two frames (context-is-king and the parts picture) run side by side and are never fused into one sentence. Second, "In the full agent picture, this is X" often reads as a filing tag, not a lens the module could not exist without.

## Narrative

**A — 92.** The story in five sentences. An engineer meets a mirror that flatters them and learns to steer it with checks and a rules file. They learn to read a plan as a draft, not a decision. They send a long task off with no package, and it comes back drifted. They diagnose the return through three lenses and send it again packaged. Then the turn: the author shows the training's own making failing in the same ways, and hands the loop over.
- M4, exercises/set-the-markers-send-it-off.md: "**The point:** send it off plainly and you find out what you can't steer yet."
- M5, exercises/diagnose-and-resend.md: "The un-packaged run was supposed to underdeliver. What came back is data, not blame."
- M6, lectures/story-of-module-6.md: "The training teaches this pattern across three modules. The agent I was working with had just finished writing those three modules. It still opened with the un-packaged shape."

The turn is the training's own failure, which meets the 100 anchor. I hold it at 92 because the M4/M5 turn depends on a session the student may not finish ("Just can't get the M5 session working? ... Despair not.").

**B — 86.** The story in five sentences. A leader who has only chatted gets a site that finally says "this is me", then builds a memory and a scheduled agent around a challenge they actually own. They hire seven agents, and the method run properly hands back an answer they cannot vouch for. Security does not cure the unease; it gives the unease a name, "residual". A benchmark and a fixed judge rescue one shape of output and then take the leader out of the loop. Sharing turns out to be absorption, not access, and the room ends with a flywheel instead of a graduation.

The turn, lived in an exercise. M3, exercises/three-minds-one-synthesis.md: "Everything you just did is the move this training teaches, run properly. It still handed you something you cannot vouch for." The method is failing on its own terms, which is close to the 100 anchor.
- M4, exercises/audit-your-agent.md: "The unease that remains is what the loop is supposed to produce, not what it failed to remove."
- M5, output-quality module: "This module is the rescue. Not full closure: your Module 3 doubt and your Module 4 residuals stay where they are."
- M8, lectures/where-is-this-all-going.md: "The kernel came out of the same move as your first briefing: agents read, agents argued, something chose, and nothing in the room checked it. Hold that doubt the way you held it then."

The mood arc (joy → unease → deeper unease → rescue → leverage) is plotted and it pays off at the close. It loses points in two places. The rescue is announced in M5's module page and its lecture before the student lives it. And the hero's first own creation, the M2 morning agent, drops out of the story.

## Stance

**A — 90.** Positions it could lose a customer over, each defended:
- "a forecast, not a measurement" (M5, lectures/what-packaging-is.md: "what it holds about your next run is a forecast ... **A prediction is not a measurement.**"). Defended by mechanism: the weights are a frozen, well-read average.
- "A rule in context is not a rule in the output" (M6, story-of-module-6.md). Defended by a scar: "four separate violations across four independent LLM instances. The grep pass caught each one."
- "A rule in memory that does not force is worse than no rule" (M6, story-of-module-6.md).
- "Don't make general what you don't practice yourself." (M3, lectures/skills-from-the-frontier.md). Defended with the Fin kit, which grew engineer by engineer.

Against its own interest: it shows its own curriculum leaking banned words and a misquote, and it refuses to name a winner ("Mollick does not call the winner. Neither does this training.", M4 pre-reads). That is a position against the training's credibility interest, not quite against its commercial interest.

**B — 87.**
- "'Share the whole agent' is a vendor pitch" (M7, exercises/share-your-work.md). Defended by mechanism in M7, access-is-not-absorption.md: "The first three copy in an afternoon. The fourth does not copy at all ... Accountability is the part with a name on it."

B holds two positions against its own commercial interest; that is a 100-anchor feature. It scores below A because several positions are defended by argument, not by a scar.
