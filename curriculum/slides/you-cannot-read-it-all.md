## You cannot read it all, so read where you know most
<!--slide:you-cannot-read-it-all-->

Agents left alone write more than anyone will read. Reading every line is not control. It is the fastest way to stop checking at page three.

Read where you know the most instead. Each of you owns a source: your interviews, your tickets, your numbers. Find the line from your own material you would trust least, and say why.

Ask for the shape you can use. A ranked list of what changed, with the source of each line, beats a confident essay.
