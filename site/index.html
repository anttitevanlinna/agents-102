Start with prework

The whole course takes about 11 hours. One message makes a game, then Claude sums up your week. You need Claude Pro or a higher paid plan.

What you need

  • Claude Pro or a higher paid plan. Pro is the minimum. The free plan is not enough. On Pro, a long module can reach the usage limit; then you pause and carry on when it resets. See Claude plans
  • A computer. The handbook is written for Claude Desktop on Mac or Windows. It also has versions for Claude Code in a terminal, Codex and GitHub Copilot.
  • A challenge you carry at work that isn't solved yet. Not a task.
  • Connecting work apps is optional. Your calendar and files help, but the course works with screenshots and saved files instead.

What you keep

The system stays in a folder on your computer: the memory of your challenge, your agents, your security skill, your judge and the loop that improves the work.

After the course, open that folder and your agents carry on with your challenge where you left off.
