## The product owner's piece: the cheapest test that could kill the bet
<!--slide:the-test-that-could-kill-it-->

Pick the cheapest test that could prove the bet wrong, and agree what would kill it before anyone sees a result.

Alex Osterwalder's Test Card (Strategyzer, 2015) makes four things explicit: what needs to be true for the idea to work, how you will test it, what you will measure, and the threshold that counts as success. Write the threshold down while nobody has a favourite yet. Below it, the hypothesis fails.

*Testing Business Ideas* (David Bland and Osterwalder, 2019) collects over forty experiments, among them Concierge, Wizard of Oz and Simple Landing Page. Agents can build a landing page in an afternoon. The card stops that cheap test from bending to the result.
