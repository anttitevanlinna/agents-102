**Sources:**
- https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/white-house-ai-framework-deadline-002011007.html — [general press] (August 1, 2026)
- https://vorplabs.com/ai-regulatory-updates/frontier-model-review-framework — [domain trade publication]

- https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF13268 — [regulatory source — CRS IF13268]
- https://explainthelaw.com/executive-orders/order/14409/ — [domain trade publication]
- https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/15/2025-00636/framework-for-artificial-intelligence-diffusion — [regulatory source]
- https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-announces-rescission-biden-era-artificial-intelligence-diffusion-rule-strengthens — [regulatory source]
- https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/alerts/bis-rescinds-its-ai-diffusion-rule-and-issues-compliance-guidance-regarding-advanced-computing-items — [practitioner analysis]
- https://www.onelexpartners.com/news-and-insights/us-export-controls-and-ai-a-practitioners-guide — [practitioner analysis]

**Date:** August 1, 2026 (deadline); August 3 (status)
**Agent level:** company (enterprise compliance planning)
**What:**

**EO 14409 (signed June 2, 2026) — frontier model review framework, NOT export control.** The August 1 deadline required three government deliverables: (1) classified AI benchmarking process (NSA/CISA/NIST), (2) voluntary frontier AI disclosure framework (30-day federal government pre-release access), (3) federal cyber workforce expansion plan. Zero deliverables published as of August 3. Yahoo Finance: "The silence is the story." Consequence: frontier AI labs are adjusting internal release timelines because they cannot determine which model architectures will trigger oversight requirements. No compliance announcements from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or Microsoft — EO targets government deliverables, not company compliance mandates.

**AI Diffusion Rule (Biden-era BIS, separate track) — legally in effect but unenforced, no replacement.** Biden-era AI export control (January 15, 2025 Federal Register) was targeted for rescission by Commerce (May 13, 2025). Rescission is incomplete: GAO decision B-337935 (May 2026) held that Commerce's non-enforcement announcement is itself a rule under the Congressional Review Act that was never submitted to Congress. No replacement rule in effect. Practitioner guidance (OneLex): "The prudent course is to continue assessing advanced-computing transactions against the rule, not to treat it as repealed." Under Secretary Kessler told House Foreign Affairs Committee (July 14, 2026) that regulatory action on AI and semiconductors "is coming." Key active controls: ECCN 3A090/4A090 chip restrictions; catch-all provisions for China/military; H200/AMD MI325X on case-by-case review for China/Macau exports.

**Evidence level:** L2 for EO 14409 deadline miss (multiple press + regulatory cross-check); L3 for AI Diffusion Rule legal status (convergence across law firm analyses + GAO decision + BIS press releases)
**Key claims:**
- EO 14409 August 1: zero deliverables published — labs uncertain on which architectures trigger oversight
- AI Diffusion Rule: legally in CFR, unenforced, no replacement — prudent to continue assessing transactions against it
- No enterprise compliance obligation from either track as of August 3
- Kessler July 14: regulatory action on AI/semiconductors "is coming" — watch H2 2026
