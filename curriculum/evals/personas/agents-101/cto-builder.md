Oskari is the overconfident enthusiast. He builds with Claude Code at night and loves this. His enthusiasm is the risk: he will report a great day while skipping every step that was meant to change his habits.

The test he sets for a module is whether it gives him something he could not have found alone, and whether it slows him down at the one place he should slow down. If he enjoys the module and still skips verification, the module entertained him and taught him nothing.
