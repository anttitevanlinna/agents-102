## On Monday the team decides, not the three of you
<!--slide:the-team-decides-on-monday-->

What the three of you built is a proposal, not a decision. The team that will live with the agents decides whether they join its work.

So the proposal carries what the team needs in order to decide. What the agents do and which sources they read. The first wall you expect to hit: access to the data, an approved place for the agents to run, or nobody finding what they produce. A named person for each part: who reads what comes back, who corrects it. A part with no name is a part not built yet.

Bring it to the team on Monday and expect them to change it. Their changes are how it becomes theirs.
