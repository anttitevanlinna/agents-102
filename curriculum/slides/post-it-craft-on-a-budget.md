## The post-it was the craft on a budget
<!--slide:post-it-craft-on-a-budget-->

An outcome on the wall. The opportunities under it on post-its. A few ideas for solutions under each. That is outcome-driven product work done right, and you were right to want it.

What the wall could not do was test anything. A real test of one idea cost a sprint of building, so a team could afford only a few a year. The rest stayed paper.

The post-it was not naive. It was what the craft shrank to when building was expensive.
