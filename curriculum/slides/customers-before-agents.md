## Without customer contact, agents build your guesses faster
<!--slide:customers-before-agents-->

Agents make building cheap. Cheap building pays off only when something tells the team which build is worth making. With no fresh evidence from customers, that something is the team's guesses. Agents build them in days, and the team ships them before any customer can say a guess was wrong.

So a team that does not talk with its customers every week should start there, before agents. Book the interviews, sit in on support calls, read what customers did last month. That sells less of this training. It is still where to start.
