## Steering raises the ceiling
<!--slide:steering-raises-the-ceiling-->

Imagine two internal mails.

Both avoid overclaiming.

One is forgettable. The other makes the reader act.

What changed?

Maybe the second mail is more executive-readable. Maybe it names the tradeoff. Maybe it gives the decision-maker a clean next move.

That dimension lives in your head right now.

Write it down.

That is a steering eval.

It asks whether the output overperformed on the dimension that matters.

Groundedness protects the floor. Steering raises the ceiling.

Do not collapse them. A grounded mail can still be useless. You need both kinds of judgment if the system is going to earn more autonomy.
