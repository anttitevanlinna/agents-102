## Generic AI is everyone's; your material is yours
<!--slide:generic-becomes-yours-->

Your competitors can use the same models you do. Ask any of them about your market and they get much the same answer you would.

What they cannot use is what your team holds: the interviews you ran, the tickets your customers filed, your own analytics, the calls you made and why. Put that in front of the agent and its answers stop being an industry take and become yours.

A quick test on anything an agent drafts: could a competitor claim this line? "Saves your team time" passes for anyone. A pattern from your own tickets does not.
