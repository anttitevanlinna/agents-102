## Sharpen the insights, never the sources
<!--slide:sharpen-insights-not-sources-->

The team's folder has three layers, kept apart on purpose.

- **Sources:** the interview transcripts, ticket exports and analytics as they came in. Nobody edits them.
- **Insight pages:** what the team believes about its customers, which the agent rewrites and sharpens as evidence arrives.
- **The rules file:** how every update is done, so each one keeps the same shape.

Let the agent rewrite a source and you lose the evidence a claim was built on. Keep the transcript as it was, and any insight can still be traced back to who said it.
