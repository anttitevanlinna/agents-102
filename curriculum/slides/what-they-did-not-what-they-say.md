## What customers did beats what they say they will do
<!--slide:what-they-did-not-what-they-say-->

An agent summarising your interviews can count "I would definitely buy that" as demand. Rob Fitzpatrick's *The Mom Test* (2013) calls that line fluff: "folks are wildly optimistic about what they would do in the future."

His three rules for a customer conversation: talk about their life instead of your idea; ask about specifics in the past instead of generics or opinions about the future; talk less and listen more.

The rules keep out compliments, which he calls worthless, fluff and opinions. What you want are facts about what people do and why. Ask the agent to keep those facts apart from the rest.
