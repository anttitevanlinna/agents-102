## The summary reads better than the evidence
<!--slide:the-source-says-less-->

An agent's summary of your interviews often reads better than the interviews behind it. That is the warning sign: to get that smooth, it can go past them. Two ways it happens.

It stretches. One ticket: a customer complained about the export. The summary: customers are unhappy with exporting. The agent used the source and claimed more than it can carry.

It smooths. Two interviews disagree: one customer wants fewer steps, another wants more control. The summary blends them into one clean need, and it hides what you most needed to see: this is not settled.

For each line a decision rests on, ask how much its source actually says.
