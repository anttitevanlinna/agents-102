## When agents analyse wider, deeper and faster, what is your insight?
<!--slide:when-agents-analyse-wider-->

An agent can read every interview transcript, every support ticket and a year of analytics in the time your team spends on one. It can sort them, find the patterns and draft the summary before you have opened the first file.

So analysis is getting cheap, the way building did. What does your team bring that the analysis does not?

Keep your own answer to that question.
