## Running notes

**AE101**

- M1 (getting-going): plants self-report-is-hypothesis (KC: "The agent's self-report is a hypothesis, not ground truth") and the compound loop (Big Idea: "Your first session should leave something behind that the next one can use"). the-machine-you-just-met.md (M1 closer) plants stance-is-ceiling ("your stance is the ceiling by construction") and names Klaassen's compound definition. compound-and-close.md's watch-for "Self-charity on rule self-review" is the FELT instance of stance-ceiling/self-charity, landing one beat before the lecture names the mechanism.
- M2 (plan-mode-done-right): double-loop learning (Argyris) changes compounding's meaning — fixing the rule that shaped the plan, not just the plan. Delegation-frontier figure appears unnamed ("What you can test and check sets your complexity ceiling").
- M3 (earn-the-trust): "Trust isn't watching harder. It's verification you can run without being there" — meaning-changes self-report-checking into built verification (skills, ADR). Team-boundary distinction changes compounding's meaning again (personal-first vs. team-worthy).
- M4 (run-the-first-experiment): no compound step by design ("this module deliberately runs no compound step, so the loop is carried rather than recited") — a structural beat on compounding, not a new one. Un-packaged send-off tests reach without full calibration, feeding delegation-frontier.
- M5 (learn-from-the-test): the-gate-is-a-claim.md is the biggest single payoff moment in the training: "Green is a claim about the check, not a fact about the work" braids self-report-is-hypothesis (M1) into gate-is-a-claim, and closes on the bitter-lesson bullet ("Today's right procedure... is superseded too... Retire what the next model outgrows, add what it needs") — explicit forward-looking payoff for both learning 1 and learning 4.
- M6 (spot-gaps-build-the-loop): "the-2-frontiers.md" echoes M1's opener, stays deliberately unanswered. Rule-cut ("Cut one rule from `./CLAUDE.local.md` the diagnosis killed") is compounding's meaning-change into subtraction. Handoff prompt generalises compounding to the whole stack — the training's actual payoff beat for learning 3.

**A101**

- M1 (getting-going): "Context is King" lecture opens the module (thesis stated before any exercise). Fabrication-catch KC: "You are the world's best evaluator of your own profile."
- M2 (building-agent-systems): "Persistence + automation = system" plants systems-compound. Three-layer context model (sources→memory→CLAUDE.md) meaning-changes context-makes-it-yours from prompt to durable file.
- M3 (multi-agent-systems): "Unsettled competence" mood plants certainty-is-unavailable explicitly in body ("The unease you feel about the synthesized briefing is not something to close today. It stays."). Multi-agent shape meaning-changes systems-compound (parallel specialisation).
- M4 (security): "Certainty is a fantasy; the discipline is the answer" meaning-changes confident-output-not-verified from personal gut-check (M1) to formal residual-risk discipline. "I can't tell" as a real answer stacks a second held doubt onto M3's.
- M5 (output-quality): benchmark-and-keep-the-winner meaning-changes verification into an empirical, measured method; "Not full closure: your Module 3 doubt and your Module 4 residuals stay where they are" is the explicit doubt-stacking move for certainty-is-unavailable.
- M6 (evaluations): "A yardstick you rewrite is not a yardstick" — fixed-judge-as-infrastructure meaning-changes confident-output-not-verified again (M5's one-off judge becomes continuous). Mollick pre-read raises the forward-looking open question, left unresolved, same device AE101 uses at M4→M5.
- M7 (personal-to-team): "Access is not absorption" is stated directly as this module's headline, largely self-contained to M7. Autonomy-ladder ("which rung has this action earned") plant continues from M5's Agent Actions section.
- M8 (agents-building-agents): "The tool that builds tools compounds" pays off systems-compound explicitly ("the flywheel that sharpened the file just now is the one that will sharpen it again after Agents 101"). "Hold that. Module 8 doesn't answer it" reuses M3's doubt-holding device at the training's actual close — the payoff beat for certainty-is-unavailable. "Humans... do not run the plumbing" is the clearest statement of the-human-moves-one-level-up.

## Where the numbers disagree with the reads

- **AE101, Learning 2 (Your stance is the ceiling).** The cold reads named this a core learning, but on the anchors it scores depth 40, applicable-in-practice 40, and self-challenging 40 — a plant-plus-one-restatement confined essentially to a single lecture (the-machine-you-just-met.md), with no later module returning to it with a changed meaning. Deciding quote: "It mirrors because mirroring was rewarded; the machine amplifies whatever posture you bring, and your stance is the ceiling by construction" (the-machine-you-just-met.md) — vivid at the moment it's said, and never developed again on the student surface.
- **AE101, Learning 5 (the open future, CANDIDATE).** As instructed, this scores as headline territory: depth 40, applicable 0, self-challenging 0. Confirms the six cold reads' choice not to name it as a carried learning. Deciding quote: "Don't answer the two frontiers. They are the arc's open questions" (the-2-frontiers.md maintainer note) — the file is built, by design, to never develop past its own opening statement.
- **A101, earned-not-announced generally.** Every A101 learning scores ≤40 on this factor (mean 33 vs. AE101's 58), against a training whose cold reads likely praised its exercise-first, discovery-driven design. The pattern: A101's Big Idea and opening-lecture lines tend to state the thesis in plain declarative prose before the exercise runs, more consistently than AE101 does. Deciding quote: "**Context is King.** You steer your agent by the context you give it. You paint by choosing the brush." (getting-going.md) — stated as the module's second line, before Exercise 1 begins.
- **A101, Learning E (Access is not absorption).** Reads may have flagged this as one of the sharpest lines in the training, but it is structurally a single-module headline: depth 40, forward-looking 0. Deciding quote: "access is not absorption. Many companies have rolled out access far ahead of trust" (personal-to-team.md) — true and quotable, but confined to M7 with no return.
