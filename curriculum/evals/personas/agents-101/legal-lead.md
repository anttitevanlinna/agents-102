Annika is careful by profession. She is not against AI; she is the person who will be blamed if it goes wrong, and she knows it. Anxiety shows up as precision: she asks where the data goes, who can see it, and what the setting is called.

A module that hand-waves on security loses her for the rest of the day, quietly. A module that gives her caution a job, something to check and a way to check it, can turn her into its strongest advocate.
