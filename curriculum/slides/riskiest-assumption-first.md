## Test the important, unproven assumption first
<!--slide:riskiest-assumption-first-->

Every product idea rests on assumptions: that customers want it, that the business should do it, that the team can build it. David Bland maps them on two axes: important or unimportant, and evidence or no evidence.

Experiment first on the ones that matter most and have the least evidence: "important and unproven", in his words. The unimportant ones can wait.

The 2x2 is older, from Lean UX; Bland added a colour for each kind of assumption. His worksheet says: "The shared understanding generated by the mapping conversation is much more valuable than the map itself."

Map them together. An agent can draft the list from your interviews and tickets. Where each one sits on the map is the team's call.
