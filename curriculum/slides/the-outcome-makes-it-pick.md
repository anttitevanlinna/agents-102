## The outcome at the root makes the merge choose
<!--slide:the-outcome-makes-it-pick-->

Three people sketching their own trees give you a spread. A spread is not an answer. Ask an agent to merge the trees with nothing else to go on, and it weighs every branch the same. Nobody chose.

Give it the outcome at the root of the tree, and the job changes. For each branch the question becomes: does this move the outcome, and what evidence says so? Branches that do not serve the outcome get dropped by name, with the reason beside them, so you can argue with the cut.

The variety comes from the three of you. The choice comes from the outcome you agreed on.
