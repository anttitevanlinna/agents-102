AE101 outcome measurement happens at day 30; no baseline response is collected or stored during training. The participant retrospectively rates before vs. now while a local agent reconstructs blinded pre/post transcript samples. The governing model is the calibrated delegation frontier: four episode states from task reach × calibration, with compounding as the trajectory mechanism. A 1–100 scale is acceptable because rubric, arithmetic, prompts, sampling, limitations, and versions are public and re-runnable. One anonymous paired record removes the need for a participant key; personal reports stay local and trainer/sponsor receive only anonymity-protected cohort aggregates. Calibration cannot be read from transcripts alone — reach is visible, but calibrated vs. reckless requires an outcome anchor. That anchor is the PR layer: review comments, merge time, and rework (review rounds requesting changes, post-merge fix commits touching the same lines, reverts). Git history also supplies a true pre-training baseline that transcripts may not.
