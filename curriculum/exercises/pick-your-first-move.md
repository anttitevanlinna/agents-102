# Exercise: Pick your first move

**Time:** 15 minutes.

**What you do:**

A week of answers has come into `responses/`, and the reader has been sorting them into `shortlists.md` as they arrived. Now you pick one move and commit to it.

## Rerun the reader and pick one move

Run the response reader over the whole week, then walk the move shortlist before you pick.

{{prompt:em-shortlist-and-first-move}}

The pick is yours. Claude lays out the answers behind each move and what it would cost, and waits. If your pick trips a Quality Gate check, Claude names the check and you decide whether the move stands.

## Commit before you know

Some first moves miss, and that is part of the work. The journal entry is what makes a miss useful: it holds what else was on the table and why this one won, so when the move lands or stalls you can see which part of your reasoning held.

Tell one person on your team which move you are starting, in the channel you already use.

## Design (EM proving run 2026-09-30)

- **Atomic — no phase markers.** one conversation; the pick and the commit are the same beat.
- **Role in M3:** first beat after Start here (Session widget lives in the module's Start here). Turns M2's week of `responses/` + `shortlists.md` into one journaled first move. Mood = conviction over correctness; "some first moves miss" is the strategy's 50%-wrong belief without the unmeasured number (writing §21).
- **Prompt:** `em-shortlist-and-first-move`. Reruns `agents/response-reader.md` (M2) over all of `responses/`, walks the move shortlist with quoted answers + cost, asks for the pick in chat, checks it against the Quality Gate (M1 backfire gates live there) before the Decision Journal entry lands. Entry fields = context · alternatives · why this won · trade-offs (strategy Block 2).
- **Manager's move:** the pick is the manager's (pedagogy §10); the prompt never picks.
- **Thin week:** fewer than three answers → entry says so, move tagged hypothesis. Handled in the fence, so no body recovery line owed.
- **Tell one person:** the move becomes real outside the file; person-to-person, no prompt (pedagogy §18). Not a reported utterance.
- **Consumed by:** `em-find-two-crux`, `em-close-find-the-two-crux`.
- **Leap test (§45), by next working day:**
  1. Tells one person on the team which move they are starting, in the team's usual channel.
  2. Has a Decision Journal entry in `team-leadership.md` naming at least two moves not taken and why.
  3. Opens `shortlists.md` after the next scheduled reader run and can say whether the pick still holds.

**View summary:** You rerun the response reader over a full week of your team's answers and commit to one first move, journaled with the moves you turned down and the trade-offs you accept.
