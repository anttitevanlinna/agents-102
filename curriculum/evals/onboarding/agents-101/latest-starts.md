# Agents 101 first-start impressions

The revised ACME workbook averaged **4.35/5 stars** over 30 simulated starts. The prior published workbook averaged **4.32/5**. The target is 4.50/5. The revised version fell short.

30 of 30 readers expected to play the first game. Each of ten synthetic personas read the handbook in three fresh calls. Every version used the same 1.0–5.0 question, with Cowork access and the demonstrations in the video captions assumed.

The first-game link opens directly at the folder step. The Mac route creates and adds the folder in one picker. The Windows route uses File Explorer. The request stays next to the instruction to paste and send it. Pause and Resume are at the end of the workbook and retain direct links.

Scores represent perceived clarity through the first game. They do not measure human task completion, elapsed time, or native Cowork behavior. The OS controls and the workbook’s direct Start and Resume links were checked in a browser.
