## An outcome is what customers do differently
<!--slide:outcomes-over-outputs-->

Josh Seiden separates what a team makes from what changes. The output is what you ship. The outcome, in his 2026 wording, is "a measurable change in human behavior that creates value". Revenue is neither: in his model it sits further out, at impact.

His warning: a product can meet every requirement and pass every test, and still create no value. Value appears only when someone uses it to do something new, or to do something they already do in a better way.

So a product box names an outcome. Not "adds a dashboard", but what a named customer will be doing differently once it works. Seiden's 2026 piece has the agent angle in its title: AI makes the output, you own the outcome.
