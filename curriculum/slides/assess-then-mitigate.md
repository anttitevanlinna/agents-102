## Assess the risks, then make one smaller
<!--slide:assess-then-mitigate-->

Risk work with agents is a loop of four steps: assess, mitigate, reassess the residual, decide. The first two, in detail.

**Assess.** List the risks. Compare what the agents can reach and what they now hold against what your team agreed could go in. What could leak? Which ordinary text could mislead them?

**Mitigate.** Pick one risk and make the smallest change that reduces it. The shapes, in product terms:

- Give it less: one folder of interviews, not the whole drive.
- Split: the agent that reads raw tickets is not the one that drafts for customers.
- Filter: names come out before a summary leaves the team.
- A person approves before anything reaches a customer.
- A second agent with a different brief reads the first one's output.
