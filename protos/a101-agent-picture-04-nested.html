PROTO A101-04 THE FULL AGENT PICTURE · NESTED Boxes inside boxes. The loop lives inside the runtime, around the model; it reaches out to context and tools; the boundary is the wall, the interface its one opening, and you stand above it.

Idea

The loop is not a track the pieces sit on; it is what the runtime does, turn after turn, around the model: read, decide, act, look at what came back, again.
From that inner unit, arms reach out to the context it reads and the tools it uses; the goal comes in at the top, the checks stand at the exit, the boundary is the wall around all of it.
The interface is the one opening in that wall, and you are on the other side of it, one level up: the wall is where you drew the line between what you give it and what you keep back.

What it cannot show

The order you added the pieces in; the figure is the finished arrangement, not the one-at-a-time build.
That other agents can sit inside the tools piece, and that memory is context the loop wrote for its next turn.
Give and keep back are shown only at the wall; the same decision inside goal and checks (how tight, how strict) stays invisible.

Where it breaks

Goal and checks both drawn through the interface imply the person hands in every goal and reads every result; a scheduled run starts and ends without you at the opening.
