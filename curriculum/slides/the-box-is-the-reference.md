## The product box keeps the agents on course
<!--slide:the-box-is-the-reference-->

Over a long piece of work, an agent drifts. The instructions it started with sit far behind it, under everything it has read since.

A reference keeps it on course: a short document the agent reads and re-reads, saying what success looks like. Your product box can be that reference: the one promise you would print on the front of the box, in the customer's words, with your press release behind it.

Point the agents at it. Before an overnight digest ranks what matters, or a new opportunity is written up, the agent compares it with the box. Work that serves nothing on the box is off course, however well it reads.

Keep the box current. A stale reference steers just as firmly.
