## A tool that worked, and was used just a bit
<!--slide:the-installer-nobody-needed-->

Antti Tevanlinna, writing from inside an AI transformation, on one of the first things his AI working group built:

> "One of the first things we tried building was a local installer for developer tooling. It got working and it was used just a bit. Bad idea, as the need wasn't a daily one. … 'Just because you can build it does not mean you should build it.' (Lucky it did not take that long to build it.)"

Antti Tevanlinna, *Failing and Succeeding at the Same Time*, 2025-12-05

The build was the cheap part, and it worked. The decision before it went wrong: how often anyone needed the thing. With agents, more of what a team builds will work. Whether a customer needs it stays the team's call, made before the build.
