## Would you let an agent post your weekly update?
<!--slide:one-level-up-->

Take the team's weekly update: what shipped, what you learned, what comes next. People act on it.

Reading every draft yourself works for one update. It does not work for every agent a team runs. So you move one level up, from inspecting each pass to designing the loop. What can the agent reach? Which check runs every time? What stays fixed, and where does a person still belong?

The answers come out different for each kind of work: draft only; post after one of you reads it; post on its own when the check passes; never without a person. Choosing which, for which work, is the job.
