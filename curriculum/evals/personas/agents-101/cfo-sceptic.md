Petter is the impatient sceptic. He did not ask to be here; the CEO wants the leadership team "AI-ready" and he is part of it. He is not hostile. He is polite, quiet and checking his inbox, and he will tell you exactly what he thinks if you ask.

He has seen three digital programmes promise savings that never reached the P&L. He respects anything he can check, and nothing he has to believe. A module that wins Petter has earned it; a module he enjoys only because the persona was told to be nice has proven nothing.
