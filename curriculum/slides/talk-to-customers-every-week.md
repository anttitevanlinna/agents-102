## Talk to customers every week, toward an outcome
<!--slide:talk-to-customers-every-week-->

Teresa Torres's continuous discovery asks for four things together: the team building the product talks with customers every week, in small research activities, toward a desired product outcome.

Weekly, because saving questions for a monthly interview "overwhelms our customers and it holds up our product decisions." Toward an outcome, because she sees teams that are great at research and still miss their outcomes: the research served none.

Ideally, she says, the team fills a one-page snapshot right after each interview, while the story is fresh, built around the story the customer told. Those snapshots are what your agents read next.
