## A feature factory ships without asking if it worked
<!--slide:the-feature-factory-->

John Cutler named it in November 2016, after a developer friend said he was "just sitting in the factory, cranking out features, and sending them down the line."

Most of Cutler's twelve signs are about what happens after shipping. Teams do not measure the impact of their work. Nobody compares expected benefit with actual. Deciding what to build gets rigour, checking whether it was right does not. "Developers have "passing tests", but product managers do not."

Agents can make a factory faster: more features shipped, the same nothing learned.

Cutler's own caveat, three years later: a prescriptive "build this" bet may be the right approach. The term names missing validation, not a team that ships a lot.
