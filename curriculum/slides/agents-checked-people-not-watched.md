## Agents get checked, people don't get watched
<!--slide:agents-checked-people-not-watched-->

Amy Edmondson defines psychological safety as a shared belief that the team is safe for interpersonal risk taking: confidence that nobody will be embarrassed, rejected or punished for speaking up. She is explicit that it is not permissiveness and not niceness. Teams where people feel it ask more, admit more, and learn more. Google's study of 180 of its own teams put it first of five things that made a team work.

With agents in the team, the most useful sentence in the room is "the agent got this wrong". It has to be safe for anyone to say it, including the newest person and including about an agent the team lead set up.

The line to hold: agents get checked, people don't get watched. A check on the agent's work is not a check on the person who ran it.
