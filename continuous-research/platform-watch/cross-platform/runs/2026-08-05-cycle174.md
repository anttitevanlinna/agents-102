## Findings

### Finding 1: Five principles were already durable; five were only scattered or absent

**What:** Review/WIP backpressure, filesystem retrieval, cognitive debt, bounded workflow judgment, and the grilling primitive already had durable coverage. Recurrence-to-lint promotion, deletion/negative knowledge, trace-first eval design, post-eval exploration, and the retrieval/prevention completion test were absent or present only as source fragments.

**Evidence level:** coverage fact about this repository, not external evidence.

**Act:** created [practitioner-operating-principles.md](../../../synthesis/practitioner-operating-principles.md) as the indexed coverage map. Each principle carries its own source type, freshness, evidence level, existing home, and next OODA question.

### Finding 2: The ten cluster into four teaching jobs, but do not form one convergent framework

**What:** The candidate clusters are throughput control, learning-system health, preserved human agency, and empirical evaluation. The clustering is our synthesis. Individual evidence ranges from L1 historical positions to the L3 cognitive-debt pattern; giving the bundle one level would launder the strongest item's evidence across the weakest.

**Evidence level:** synthesis only; no field-level claim.

**Counter-evidence:** Klaassen's current guide says parallelization shifts the bottleneck toward compute, while Ronacher says accountable review remains the bottleneck. Willison's delegated-judgment technique also pulls against prescriptive workflow rules. These tensions are useful; they prevent the synthesis from becoming a tidy doctrine practitioners do not actually share.

### Finding 3: Freshness and outcome evidence are the main gaps

**What:** Ronacher's deletion account and Husain/Shankar's eval method are outside the six-month currency window. Pocock's grilling and Klaassen's retrieval test are current, but independent outcome evidence is missing. The research can support a curriculum discussion about mechanisms; it cannot yet claim these are current field standards or that each intervention improves delivery.

**Evidence level:** L1–L2 gap assessment.

## What I looked for but did not find

- A fresh Ronacher restatement of the 2025 delete-unused-automation rule.
- A 2026 named-team account showing an observed trace taxonomy changing its eval suite.
- An independent team measuring rework before and after Pocock-style grilling.
- A team measuring whether compounded guidance was retrieved and prevented recurrence later.
- Nordic practitioner evidence for any of the four candidate clusters.

## Orientation

The research system now has enough source discipline to discuss curriculum placement without arguing from memory. It does not yet support a ten-item theory dump: several items are single-practitioner methods, two are historically sourced, and the most interesting practitioner positions conflict on how much judgment should be encoded versus delegated.
