## A flat score shows where the check stops seeing
<!--slide:a-flat-score-->

A check scores research summaries round after round. The problems it flags drop toward zero and stay there. It is tempting to read that as finished.

The check measures one thing, say whether every claim traces to an interview. Once the agent stops writing untraced claims, the check has nothing left to flag. The summaries can still be getting sharper, or staying vague, in directions it was never built to see.

A flat score tells you about what the check can see, and nothing about the rest. When the number stops moving, your own judgement comes back in: what does this check not look at?
