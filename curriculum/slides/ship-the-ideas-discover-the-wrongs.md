## A digest that came back wrong is the discovery
<!--slide:ship-the-ideas-discover-the-wrongs-->

Antti Tevanlinna, *Expect to Be Wrong with the Vision and Ideas*, 2025-05-09:

> "I used to think of being wrong as failure. But being wrong does not diminish you as a person. Lack of effort to understand does."

> "Ship the ideas — Discover the wrongs"

He prefers a version of Plan-Do-Check-Act whose last two steps read "Check the results with somebody" and "Create new theory. Create new understanding."

Expect an agent's first digest of your customer material to be wrong in places. That is the check working early, on something cheap.
