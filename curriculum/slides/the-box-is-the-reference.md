## The product box is what the agents re-read
<!--slide:the-box-is-the-reference-->

Over a long piece of work, an agent drifts. The instructions it started with sit far behind it, under everything it has read since.

A reference keeps it on course: a short document the agent reads and re-reads, saying what success looks like. Your product box and press release can be that reference. One face, one promise, in the customer's words.

Point the agents at it. Before an overnight digest ranks what matters, or a new opportunity goes on the list, the agent checks it against the box. Work that serves nothing on the box is off course, however well it reads.

Keep the box current. A stale reference steers just as firmly.
