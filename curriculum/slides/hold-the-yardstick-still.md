## A rising score counts only if the check held still
<!--slide:hold-the-yardstick-still-->

An agent rewrites your onboarding emails over three rounds, and a check scores each round against what your team wrote down as good. The score climbs.

If someone tightened or loosened the check between rounds, the climb could mean the emails got better or the check got easier. From the outside the two look identical, and you cannot tell which one moved.

So fix the check before the first round and leave it alone until the last. Change the work, the agent's instructions, the examples. When the check itself needs to change, start a new run and score from a fresh baseline.
