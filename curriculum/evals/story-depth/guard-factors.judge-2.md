**M4 Security.** C's biggest restatement, meaning changed to a named discipline: `practice-of-risk.md` — "Certainty is a fantasy you inherited," "Run the loop. You never finish. Self-challenging: "'I can't tell' is a real answer." `module-5-prework.md` gives B its sharpest single line: "It can verify inside the same fiction. **M5 Grounded output.** B restated/generalized from the Mata case: `grounded.md` — "'Are you sure?' is another fluent answer" as a general design principle, plus "A drift signal, never proof" (training doubts its own consistency-check as a method). Module body: "Which rung has this action earned?" / "Propose, double-check, apply."

**M6 Evaluations.** D's major payoff: `new-human-role-in-the-loop.md` — "The human moves one level up," "Designing the conditions under which the system earns more autonomy, one loop at a time." Self-challenging at its sharpest, training shows its own mechanism's silent failure mode: `when-the-score-stops-moving.md` — "You haven't checked the judge yet... F: "the fan-out shape, now running a quality discipline instead of a content discipline." Forward-seed for both B and F: "If the model stops fabricating, what is your judge for?... That is the question the next lecture opens" (picked up structurally, not directly answered, by M8's closing question).

**M7 Personal to team.** E's Big Idea and payoff module: "You can't really share an agent... The hard part is not access; it is absorption." `access-is-not-absorption.md` layers three distinct meaning-changing beats: "You cannot share an agent" (accountability doesn't copy), "Access is easy; absorption is scarce," and "People absorb what they already half know" (Polanyi tacit-knowledge mechanism, new causal layer under the same claim).

**M8 Agents building agents.** F's Big Idea: "The tool that builds tools compounds." Closing lecture `where-is-this-all-going.md` braids and pays off multiple threads at once: C+B braided and closed — "the kernel came out of the same move as your first briefing: agents read, agents argued, something chose, and nothing in the room checked it. Hold that doubt the way you held it then"; E echoed — "Count the folders the kernel cites... Access was instant and complete. What the room took up from each other is the smaller number"; D extended — "You wrote down what an agent should do and you did not build it... You moved from writing the assembly to specifying it"; F's forward-looking payoff, the strongest hit in the corpus for the 100-anchor — "Ask it of the kernel your room just produced. *What would have to show up for us to drop this?*"
