## Sharing what you built is harder than building it
<!--slide:sharing-is-harder-than-building-->

> "We built good things. We failed to share them well. … Agents can dissolve the technical barriers. The social and organisational barriers are still there."

Antti Tevanlinna, *What I Know Now That I Didn't Know Then*, 2026

Months earlier he had named his own part in it:

> "We made an 80% ready vision, but I never pushed enough to put it really on paper and share. … When you do stuff that you don't know, you are bound to fumble. But it still feels so bad sometimes."

Antti Tevanlinna, *Failing and Succeeding at the Same Time*, 2025-12-05

What three people worked out stays with three people until someone writes it where the team will find it and asks the team to decide. Plan that step as part of the work.
