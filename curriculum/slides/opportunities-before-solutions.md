## A solution can wear an opportunity's clothes
<!--slide:opportunities-before-solutions-->

"I want to go out to eat" sounds like a customer need. It is already a choice. Teresa Torres's test for a branch on the opportunity tree is a question: is there more than one way to address this? If not, it is a solution wearing an opportunity's clothes.

In her tree the outcome sits at the root. Under it are opportunities: in her words, an unmet customer need, pain point, or desire. Under each hang the solutions that might address it.

With agents, three rough solutions cost an afternoon. Knowing which opportunity matters still takes listening to customers.
