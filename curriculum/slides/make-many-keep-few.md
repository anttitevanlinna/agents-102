## Make many, keep few, remember what survived
<!--slide:make-many-keep-few-->

Agents make rough work cheap, so make more of it than you need: ten rough prototype directions instead of one polished one, five wordings of a fake-door page, three readings of the same interviews.

Then something has to throw most of it away. A check against what good means, a customer in a test, the three of you making the call. Selection is where your judgement goes in.

What survives gets written down, in the agents' memory, in the rules they follow, in the team's notes. The next round starts from there instead of from zero.
