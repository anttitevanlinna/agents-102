## Cheap building helps your rivals too
<!--slide:where-the-frame-breaks-->

> "One company becomes twice as effective at creating new features. The other companies have no choice but to follow. … One 'absurdum' about the future is that 1000++ features in a product start to confuse the user more than add value."

Antti Tevanlinna, *The Red Queen*, 2026-03-20

This is where "building got cheap, deciding didn't" stops being an edge on its own. When rivals build as cheaply, speed is the entry price, and a product that adds everything it can build gets harder to use.

What would change this training's mind: agents that start deciding well, choosing the outcome and reading a no as clearly as a yes. Then the question of what each of you is for opens again.
