## It found what you asked it to look for
<!--slide:it-found-what-you-asked-->

Open the brief you wrote for the digest agent. Find the sentence that told it what to look for. Does it name your hypothesis, as something to find evidence for?

If it does, the agent did as asked. Ask an agent to look for something in enough customer conversations and it will usually find a few. What your brief never asked is how many customers said something else, or what would show the hypothesis wrong.

So the agreement may come from your question, not from your customers. Ask again the other way round: what in this material argues against our hypothesis, and how much of it is there?
