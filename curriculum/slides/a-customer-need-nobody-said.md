## A customer quote no customer said
<!--slide:a-customer-need-nobody-said-->

The most dangerous thing an agent writes in product work is not a wrong number. It is a quote: a customer need, in your users' own voice, that sounds exactly like the interviews and that nobody ever said.

It fits. It agrees with what the team already suspected, reads like research, and lands in the opportunity tree with the same weight as the real interviews around it. A week later someone builds for it.

The person who can catch it is the one who sat in the interviews. So every quote in the tree carries its source: which interview, which person, which minute.
