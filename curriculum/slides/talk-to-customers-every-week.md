## Great research can still miss the outcome
<!--slide:talk-to-customers-every-week-->

Teresa Torres often sees teams who are great at research and still don't meet their outcomes. Her reason: "their research isn't in service of a desired outcome."

So her continuous discovery asks for four things together: the team building the product talks with customers every week, in small research activities, toward a desired product outcome. Weekly, because saving questions for a monthly interview "overwhelms our customers and it holds up our product decisions."

Ideally, she says, the team fills a one-page snapshot right after each interview, while the story is fresh. Those snapshots are what your agents read next.
