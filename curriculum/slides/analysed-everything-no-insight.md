## It analysed everything and still had no insight
<!--slide:analysed-everything-no-insight-->

The digest read every interview, every ticket and every number your team let it reach. It counted mentions, grouped themes and ranked them. That is more reading than your team has time for.

None of it is a choice. The digest cannot tell you which of its findings matters, because that depends on what your team is trying to do, and for whom. When it agrees with your favourite hypothesis, that is your own guess handed back, not an insight.

When agents analyse wider, deeper and faster than you can, analysis stops being the scarce part. So what is the insight only your team can bring?
