## Criteria keep a floor and can raise a ceiling
<!--slide:the-floor-and-the-ceiling-->

Some criteria protect the floor. Every customer quote traces to an interview. Every number traces to its report. Every claim points to evidence or says what is missing. Work that passes is attached to the ground. It can still be useless.

Imagine two opportunity summaries, both grounded. One is forgettable. The other names the tradeoff, keeps the customer's own words and makes the next decision obvious.

That difference lives in someone's head: the designer who rewrites every persona, the product owner who knows which customer to call. Write it down, and it becomes criteria the agents can aim at.

The floor keeps the work honest. The ceiling makes it worth reading.
