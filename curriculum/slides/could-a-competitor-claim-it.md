## A line a competitor could claim is still generic
<!--slide:could-a-competitor-claim-it-->

Read your product box and press release one line at a time, and ask: could a competitor claim this?

"Saves your team time." Anyone can print that. "Product managers stop rebuilding the quarterly report from four spreadsheets." That one needs someone who has listened to your customers.

Every line a competitor could claim is a line where your box is still generic. Mark those lines. Then ask what you know that they don't: a quote from an interview, a pattern in the tickets, a number from your own analytics. That is what sharpens the line.

The same test works on anything an agent drafts for you.
