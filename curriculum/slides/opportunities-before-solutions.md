## Opportunities before solutions
<!--slide:opportunities-before-solutions-->

Teresa Torres draws discovery as a tree. The outcome sits at the root. Under it are opportunities: in her words, an unmet customer need, pain point, or desire. Under each opportunity hang the solutions that might address it.

Her test for a branch is a question: is there more than one way to address this? If not, it is a solution wearing an opportunity's clothes. "I want to go out to eat" sounds like a need. It is already a choice.

She asks teams to set several solutions against each other for the opportunity they pick, because comparing options makes better decisions than judging one. Agents make that cheap. Three rough solutions cost an afternoon now. Judging which opportunity matters still costs what it always did: listening to customers.
