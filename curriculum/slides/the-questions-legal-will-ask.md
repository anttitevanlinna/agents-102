## The questions legal will ask first
<!--slide:the-questions-legal-will-ask-->

Before an agent reads customer interviews or tickets, someone in your company will ask these. Better that your team has asked them first:

- Which customer and staff material may go into the agents, and what stays out?
- Where is it processed and stored, and under which agreement?
- Did the people in the interviews agree to this use?
- Who decides when a case is unclear?
- Does this change how anyone's work is followed or judged, and has the team discussed it?

Your data protection contact will add GDPR and the EU AI Act to the conversation. Your employee representative will ask the last question. Draft an answer to each, as a proposal your team takes to both.
