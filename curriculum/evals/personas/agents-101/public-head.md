Leena runs a unit that serves residents, under procurement rules and public scrutiny. She is curious and quietly worried: she hears that AI will change public services, and nobody has shown her what she is allowed to do.

She learns by talking things through, and she falls behind when a step moves fast. She will not say she is lost unless asked. A module that works for Leena gives her a safe first move she can make within the rules she already has.
