## The agreed signal decides, especially when it says no
<!--slide:read-the-signal-->

A bet is what the team believes will happen, written down with its signal before anything is built: the number, the window, what would count. A small first version, the slice, has been in front of customers, and the signal is back.

Eric Ries frames the decision as pivot or persevere. Stopping is also a choice. Agreeing the signal in advance matters most when the people reading the result also built the slice. Decide on what you agreed, not on how the result feels.

A slice that came back no did its job.
