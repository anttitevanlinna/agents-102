## A list of don'ts does not teach good work
<!--slide:show-what-good-looks-like-->

If your agents' rules grow only from corrections, they turn into a list of don'ts. A prohibition is a weak instruction: it stops one behaviour and puts nothing in its place.

"Don't invent customer quotes" says what to avoid. "Every quote names the interview it came from, like this one" shows what to do. The positive form steers in cases no prohibition saw coming.

So when an agent gets something exactly right, keep it as an example of good. A digest that ranked what changed. A summary that kept two sources' disagreement in plain view. Those belong in the rules beside the corrections.
