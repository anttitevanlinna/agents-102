# AE101 M9 Round 1 — named-practitioner scan

**Question:** How do current practitioners turn coding-agent sessions, traces, and human corrections into durable knowledge that improves later runs?

**Checked:** 2026-09-06. Moving-world cutoff: 2026-03-06. Person-specific primary-source search only.

## Verdict

No named practitioner in this sweep demonstrates the complete M9 chain end to end: coding-session/trace ingestion → candidate-rule harvest → explicit human promotion → durable agent-readable store → measured improvement on a comparable later coding task. The closest implementations split the chain in two. Every has a concrete coding-session capture, persistence, maintenance, and retrieval system, but only an anecdotal later-use result and no cross-run effectiveness measure. Husain and Shankar have a concrete human-approved trace-to-taxonomy-to-eval system with held-out measurement, but do not show it promoting coding-session lessons into repo rules or skills. [Every implementation](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/docs/guides/ce-compound.md) `[practitioner direct]`; [Husain and Shankar](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/how-many-examples-do-i-need-for-an-eval.html) `[practitioner direct]`.

This is an **L2-at-best composition hypothesis**, not L3 convergence. None of the positive mechanisms clears all three finding gates: the work is agentic, but independent replication plus a named, measurable cross-run outcome is absent. The limits have only N=3 independent current practitioner voices (Osmani, Ronacher, Böckeler), below the 10–20 L3 threshold. [Osmani](https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/) `[practitioner direct]`; [Ronacher](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/6/23/the-coming-loop/) `[practitioner direct]`; [Böckeler](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/sensors-for-coding-agents.html) `[practitioner direct]`.

## Mechanisms

### 1. Klaassen / Every — session history to repo learning

- **Captured:** one solved, verified, non-obvious problem per invocation. Full mode probes recent Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Pi, and omp sessions, keeps at most five relevant sessions, and extracts attempted approaches, errors, decisions, and user corrections; the current session supplies the fresh problem context. [`session-history.md`](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/skills/ce-compound/references/session-history.md) `[practitioner direct]`; [`session-historian.md`](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/skills/ce-compound/references/agents/session-historian.md) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Stored:** `<docs_root>/solutions/<category>/<slug>.md` with YAML metadata; session-derived dead ends go into `What Didn't Work` or `Context`. High-overlap findings update the existing canonical learning instead of creating a duplicate. Qualifying vocabulary may also update `CONCEPTS.md`. [`assembly.md`](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/skills/ce-compound/references/assembly.md) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Promotion owner:** in Klaassen's manual polish flow, the human notices three or four reusable changes and invokes `ce-compound`. In configured automatic mode, the agent applies the non-obvious/durable/material counterfactual and writes the learning without a per-learning approval; interactive consent is reserved for adding a discoverability pointer to `AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md`, and ambiguous refresh actions. [Klaassen](https://every.to/source-code/how-i-polish-software-that-agents-built) `[practitioner direct]`; [`ce-compound` guide](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/docs/guides/ce-compound.md) `[practitioner direct]`; [`refresh-and-discoverability.md`](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/skills/ce-compound/references/refresh-and-discoverability.md) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Later consumption:** the instruction-file pointer makes the store discoverable; `ce-plan` and `ce-ideate` search `solutions/`. Klaassen reports one completed reuse event: after he corrected an animation's direction and compounded the preference, a later card feature used the preferred animation without another prompt. [Every guide](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/docs/guides/ce-compound.md) `[practitioner direct]`; [Klaassen](https://every.to/source-code/how-i-polish-software-that-agents-built) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Measurement:** the shipped validators check frontmatter, links, cited code behavior, and merge-state claims. **Inference from the implementation:** this validates the learning artifact, not its effect on later-task quality, correction count, or time-to-success against an untreated baseline. Klaassen's animation is a single completed event, not a current typical-effect estimate. [Every guide](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/docs/guides/ce-compound.md) `[practitioner direct]`; [Klaassen](https://every.to/source-code/how-i-polish-software-that-agents-built) `[practitioner direct]`. **Evidence: L2 single-team mechanism; no independent measurable replication.**

### 2. Husain / Shankar — traces to human-grounded failure knowledge

- **Captured:** a trace is treated as the full record of a user session. Their current prescription starts with diverse traces and free-text human notes; the first pass stays manual so agent suggestions do not bias discovery. After the human has established examples, the agent searches for similar failures. [Husain and Shankar](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/how-many-examples-do-i-need-for-an-eval.html) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Stored:** the coding-agent skill builds an annotation app and persists `samples.json`, `annotations.json`, `patterns.json`, `suggestions.json`, and a graph under `error_discovery_data/`; the accumulated failure taxonomy and confirmed examples become the durable knowledge. [`error-discovery/SKILL.md`](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/11d35781d43c281baddd3c6a766b12d81c274c29/skills/error-discovery/SKILL.md) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Promotion owner:** agent suggestions are visibly separate from human annotations and require accept/dismiss. The human's free-text judgment is ground truth; the agent organizes it and proposes coverage. [`review-loop.md`](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/11d35781d43c281baddd3c6a766b12d81c274c29/skills/error-discovery/review-loop.md) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Later consumption:** the taxonomy decides which failures deserve deterministic checks or LLM judges; confirmed failures and important workflows enter a repeatable eval set that is rerun when the application changes. [Husain and Shankar](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/how-many-examples-do-i-need-for-an-eval.html) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Measurement:** their method validates an inferential judge against domain-expert labels using dev/test splits and TPR/TNR, with one held-out test run; deterministic checks use ordinary tests. The round-number sample sizes and thresholds are recommendations, not reported outcome rates from a named deployment. [`validate-evaluator/SKILL.md`](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/11d35781d43c281baddd3c6a766b12d81c274c29/skills/validate-evaluator/SKILL.md) `[practitioner direct]`; [Husain and Shankar](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/how-many-examples-do-i-need-for-an-eval.html) `[practitioner direct]`. **Evidence: L1 current practitioner method; no named coding-team outcome.**

### 3. Osmani — skills plus an external state spine

Osmani's loop stores project knowledge in skills and stores attempted/passed/open work in Markdown or Linear outside the conversation; later scheduled work reads those artifacts rather than re-deriving the project. He keeps human ownership of verification and explicitly warns that the maker model is charitable toward its own work and that smooth loops accelerate comprehension loss if the human stops reading. He does not expose a correction-harvest, promotion-review, or cross-run learning metric in this account. [Osmani](https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/) `[practitioner direct]`. **Evidence: L1 first-hand operating pattern, no specific outcome.**

### 4. Böckeler — promote known failure modes into executable sensors

Böckeler encodes known agent failure modes as deterministic rules and enriches error messages with self-correction guidance. Her sensor stack runs during coding sessions, repeats the computational checks in CI, and runs other checks on slower schedules; she logs sensor state each time it is checked so later analysis can ask whether violations trend down, which rules never fire, and which fail often enough to justify better guidance. In her internal dashboard experiment, the agent violated dependency rules a handful of times and self-corrected from the sensor output; she reports improved review experience and trust, not a quantified cross-run quality delta. [Böckeler](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/sensors-for-coding-agents.html) `[practitioner direct]`. **Evidence: L2 exploratory single case; outcome remains qualitative.**

### 5. Ronacher — durable traces are not themselves durable learning

Ronacher describes Pi session logs as persisted data that is opened, branched, compacted, exported, shared, and analyzed. In Pi's own development, the agent repeatedly responds to local failures by adding permissive readers and defensive complexity; maintainers must pull it back to the global invariant. No trace-to-rule promotion path or later-run measure is described. [Ronacher, *Building Pi With Pi*](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/5/24/pi-oss/) `[practitioner direct]`. **Evidence: L2 first-hand case for the limit, not for self-improvement.**

## Counter-evidence and limits

- **Rule bloat / context pollution:** Every's current durable bar rejects anything recoverable from code, tests, types, comments, or existing docs, updates high-overlap learnings in place, and runs targeted refresh for stale or contradictory guidance. **Inference from those safeguards:** indiscriminate capture is treated as capable of making the store worse. [Every guide](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/docs/guides/ce-compound.md) `[practitioner direct]`. Böckeler's February account, historical because it falls outside the moving-world window, says copied context tends to overengineering, repetition, and contradiction and should be built iteratively. [Böckeler, 2026-02-05](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/context-engineering-coding-agents.html) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Feedback pollution:** Böckeler observed each added rule set produce a mixture of relevant and irrelevant findings; interacting max-lines constraints pushed complexity into component-property chains, creating a risk of feedback overload and over-engineered refactoring. [Böckeler](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/sensors-for-coding-agents.html) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Self-review charity:** Osmani separates maker and checker because the model that wrote the code is too lenient on itself; Husain/Shankar do not accept judge output until it has been compared with human labels on held-out data. [Osmani](https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/) `[practitioner direct]`; [Husain and Shankar](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/11d35781d43c281baddd3c6a766b12d81c274c29/skills/validate-evaluator/SKILL.md) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Loop success does not imply learning:** Ronacher reports little success with unattended harness loops for code he cares about; he sees outputs that remain too defensive, complex, and locally reasoned, with little progress on those traits. [Ronacher](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/6/23/the-coming-loop/) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **A trace corpus can narrow discovery:** Husain/Shankar keep the first pass manual, include random samples, and require re-review because early agent suggestions bias human judgment and reviewer criteria drift over time. [Husain and Shankar](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/how-many-examples-do-i-need-for-an-eval.html) `[practitioner direct]`; [`review-loop.md`](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/11d35781d43c281baddd3c6a766b12d81c274c29/skills/error-discovery/review-loop.md) `[practitioner direct]`.

## What was not found

- No named-practitioner source with a controlled before/after on two comparable coding tasks after one harvested correction was promoted. Klaassen's later animation reuse is the only specific cross-session outcome found, and it is one anecdote from one product. [Klaassen](https://every.to/source-code/how-i-polish-software-that-agents-built) `[practitioner direct]`.
- No explicit, measured threshold for promoting several incident learnings into a broad repo rule or pattern doc. Every defines pattern docs as generalizations from several learnings and warns that they are higher-risk when stale, but the inspected flow does not publish an effectiveness-tested promotion rule. [`CONCEPTS.md`](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/CONCEPTS.md) `[practitioner direct]`.
- No fresh Addy Osmani implementation that mines session transcripts or corrections into skills; his current account starts after knowledge has already been encoded in a skill or state file. [Osmani](https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/) `[practitioner direct]`.
- No Böckeler or Ronacher source in the window that automatically promotes trace findings into agent instructions. Böckeler manually designs executable sensors; Ronacher preserves/analyzes sessions but continues to correct global invariants manually. [Böckeler](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/sensors-for-coding-agents.html) `[practitioner direct]`; [Ronacher](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/5/24/pi-oss/) `[practitioner direct]`.
- No standalone fresh Shreya Shankar coding-agent case beyond the joint Husain/Shankar eval materials. Their joint system can review agent traces and code, but the published outcome is a method and toolchain rather than a named deployment result. [Husain and Shankar](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/how-many-examples-do-i-need-for-an-eval.html) `[practitioner direct]`; [`evals-skills`](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills) `[practitioner direct]`.

## Provisional evidence call

- **Durable coding-session capture and retrieval:** L2 single-team mechanism (Every); fails independent replication and measurable-outcome gates. [Every](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/docs/guides/ce-compound.md) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Human-approved trace-to-failure-taxonomy-to-eval:** L1 current method (Husain/Shankar); technically explicit, but no named deployment/outcome in the source. [Husain and Shankar](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/how-many-examples-do-i-need-for-an-eval.html) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Executable feedback as durable knowledge:** L2 exploratory single case (Böckeler); same-run self-correction observed, cross-run improvement not quantified. [Böckeler](https://www.martinfowler.com/articles/sensors-for-coding-agents.html) `[practitioner direct]`.
- **Full M9 proposition:** below reporting threshold. Teach only as an experiment whose outcome is the diff between two runs, not as an established practitioner standard. The evidence-backed safety shape is human-first error discovery, selective promotion, independent checks, a discoverable versioned store, stale-rule maintenance, and an explicit no-write outcome when the session earned nothing. [Husain and Shankar](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/how-many-examples-do-i-need-for-an-eval.html) `[practitioner direct]`; [Every](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/b159e1fa4c70efa995742269d38269bcc7524dd2/docs/guides/ce-compound.md) `[practitioner direct]`; [Osmani](https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/) `[practitioner direct]`.
