## Why the LLM fabricates
<!--slide:why-the-llm-fabricates-->

- **It completes the pattern.** An LLM continues text in the form that looks right. A legal brief or consulting report calls for case names and citations, so they arrive whether or not the fact exists.
- **It fills gaps instead of stopping.** When the sources miss a piece, the model supplies what makes the answer feel complete.
- **It verifies inside the same fiction.** Asking the same model "are you sure?" is another fluent answer, not a check on the world. Verification has to leave the generation loop and touch the source.
- **It inherits organisational shortcuts.** If the workflow rewards speed and has no step where someone opens the cited source, the fabrication survives.
