## A file the team writes once steers every answer
<!--slide:a-file-the-team-writes-once-->

A message you type shapes one answer, then it is gone. A file the agent reads each time it starts work shapes every answer after it.

What your product is for, who the customer is, the words your customers use and the ones they never would, the sources you trust: write them down once and nobody retypes them.

Each of you sees the product from a different seat. The product owner knows the outcome, the designer the customer's own words, the team lead what the agents may touch. A file written by all three of you steers better than one written by whoever got there first.
