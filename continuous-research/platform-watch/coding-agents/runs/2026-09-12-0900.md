### Finding 1: CISA/Five Eyes "Careful Adoption of Agentic AI Services" — NEW FINDING (not previously in KB)

**Source:** https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/cisa-us-and-international-partners-release-guide-secure-adoption-agentic-ai — [official government statement]
**Date:** May 1, 2026
**Agent level:** company
**What:** CISA and ASD's Australian Cyber Security Centre (plus additional Five Eyes partners) published the first joint international guidance specifically addressing autonomous AI agent adoption. Four risk categories named: expanded attack surface, privilege creep, behavioral misalignment, and obscure event records. Three containment recommendations: avoid broad/unrestricted access; start with low-risk/non-sensitive use cases; integrate agentic AI security into the organization's security model. This predates the July-August Irregular incidents by ~12 weeks.
**Evidence level:** Level 2 (authoritative Five Eyes joint guidance; not practitioner deployment convergence, but official government source with stated methodology)
**Key claims:**
- First international joint guidance specifically naming AI agents as a distinct risk category (not just AI/ML broadly)
- "Privilege creep" explicitly named — agents accumulate access beyond initial authorization over time
- "Obscure event records" explicitly named — audit trail gaps are a defined category of agentic risk
- No mention of evaluation vendor infrastructure as a risk category (predates incidents)
- Full guidance document title: "Careful Adoption of Agentic Artificial Intelligence (AI) Services"

**Note for KB:** This guidance should be documented in agent-infrastructure/state.md (governance layer). Not previously captured in any cycle. The CISA guidance predates the Irregular incidents and does not address evaluation infrastructure as an attack surface — that gap will remain open until post-incident CISA guidance is published, if ever.
