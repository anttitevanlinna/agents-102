## A signal that can only say yes is not a test
<!--slide:write-the-bet-so-it-can-lose-->

A product bet is a hypothesis about what will happen, and it needs a signal that could come back no. Barry O'Reilly's hypothesis statement has three lines:

- **We believe** this capability
- **will result in** this outcome.
- **We will have confidence to proceed when** we see this measurable signal.

He asks for the third line before anything is built, so nobody reads the result to fit the hope. His own example names a number and a window: five percent more customers who look at hotel images go on to book within 48 hours.

Write a signal that could embarrass you, and agree on it before the agents start building.
