Here are all five StoryBrand beats in one go. Use them and regenerate without asking one at a time:
- Character: a thoughtful adult who wants psychologically sound help with attention or stress, without being treated like a diagnosis.
- Problem: advice online is either clinical jargon or breezy self-help certainty. Neither helps them decide what to try on Monday.
- Guide: I am a licensed psychologist who translates research carefully and keeps ordinary human difficulty ordinary.
- Plan: start with one understandable idea, show where it applies and where it stops, then offer one small experiment.
- Success: the reader understands themselves better and can make one kinder, more deliberate choice.
My guide line is: “Clear enough to use, careful enough to trust.”
