PROTO A101-01 THE FULL AGENT PICTURE · ANATOMY The eight pieces as physical parts of one machine around the model, each carrying the same give / keep-back dial. For the slide "The full picture, and what it is made of".

Idea

An agent is a body built around the model: context surrounds it, tools reach out of it, the loop orbits it, checks gate what leaves it, the goal is what it aims at, the boundary fences all of it, and the interface is the one opening where you stand.

Every part wears the same dial, so the eye reads eight decisions, not eight components.

The dials sit at different settings on purpose: nobody gives everything or keeps everything back.

What it cannot show

The order the parts arrived in: the picture is assembled, not built up, so the "one at a time" in the paragraph has to be said aloud.

That context grows into memory across sessions, and that other agents sit inside the tools; both collapse into their parent part.

What a dial setting means for any one part; the dial is a shape for the decision, not a scale.

Where it breaks

The dial suggests a single slider; for the boundary and the interface the real decision is several yes/no choices, and a literal reader may ask what "60% interface" means.
