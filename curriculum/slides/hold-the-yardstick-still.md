## A rising score counts only if the criteria held still
<!--slide:hold-the-yardstick-still-->

An agent rewrites your onboarding emails over three rounds, and each round is scored against what your team wrote down as good. The score climbs.

If someone changed the criteria between rounds, the climb could mean the emails got better or the criteria got easier. You cannot tell which.

So fix the criteria before the first round and leave them alone until the last. Change the work, the agent's instructions, the examples. When the criteria themselves need to change, start again and score every round against the new version.
