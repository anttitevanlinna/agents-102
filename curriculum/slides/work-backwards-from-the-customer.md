## Work backwards from the customer
<!--slide:work-backwards-from-the-customer-->

Amazon teams write the press release before they build. Colin Bryar and Bill Carr, who ran the practice there, call it **working backwards**: a short release in the customer's own words, then the questions a customer would ask, then the questions your leaders would ask. Their phrase for the release is a *forcing function* to keep the idea focused on the customer.

The release is not marketing. Its readers are the people deciding whether to build. One of the questions it has to answer is why the product will fail.

The box on your table is the same move in cardboard: one face, one promise, in words a customer would repeat. Luke Hohmann's original game hands the box to customers and lets them sell it to each other. Here your team builds it, and the test is the same. Would a customer pick it up?
