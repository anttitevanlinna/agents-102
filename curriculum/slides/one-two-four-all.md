## Hear every idea before anything gets merged
<!--slide:one-two-four-all-->

1-2-4-All, from Liberating Structures, takes about a quarter of an hour. One minute alone, in silence, writing ideas down. Two minutes in pairs, building on them while keeping each idea distinct. Five minutes in fours, refining them and telling them apart. Then all together, where a few people share something everyone should hear.

The numbers are group sizes, not minutes. Its authors, Henri Lipmanowicz and Keith McCandless, say it "engages every voice regardless of group size" and brings out "ideas people would otherwise keep to themselves."

Run it before an agent merges anything. Each person's ideas are already written down, with a name on them, so the one only you had is on the page before anything gets averaged.
