## Story spine

The participant is the hero; Claude and the trainer are guides (StoryBrand). One question carries the three days, from the participant's seat: **what is each of us for, when building gets cheap?** Planted on Day 1 (the box: what the product is for), complicated on Day 2 (the agent makes things up in your users' voice; who catches it), paid off on Day 3 (each role's own piece, laid side by side; the proposal to the team).

## Theory spine: the outcome loop

Outcome → opportunities → bet → slice → signal → back to the outcome. The product craft always ran this loop; building cost made each turn slow. Agents make building cheap, so the loop turns faster, and the craft decides where it turns. Control is the signal side of the loop (what came back, what we trust). Creativity is the bet side (what we try, what each of us makes). Together is who runs the loop: the team, not one power user.
