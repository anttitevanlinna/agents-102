## "The agent got this wrong" costs nobody face
<!--slide:the-agent-got-this-wrong-->

Tell a colleague "your summary is wrong" and you are judging their work in front of the team. Say "the agent got this wrong" and what gets questioned is the agent's output, not a person.

That makes the sentence cheap to say, and it has to stay cheap: for the newest person in the team, and about the team lead's own agent too. A digest nobody dares question gets checked by nobody.

So say it out loud, early, about anything an agent produced. The person who ran the agent can join the check instead of defending it.
