## The models will analyse wider, deeper and faster. What will your insight be?
<!--slide:what-will-your-insight-be-->

The models your agents run on are your rivals' models too. What none of them can hand you is the call: which customer, which bet, which no, and what a no that comes back means for the next bet.

So the question stays open, and it is yours to answer on your own product. Which insight do the three of you hold that no analysis gave you? Write it where your team will see it, and put it to the test on Monday.
