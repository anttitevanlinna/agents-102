# From Personal to Team

## Big Idea

You can't really share an agent. You can share its context, a skill, its output or an interface, and choosing between them is a strategy decision.

## Prework

Bring one example from your own organisation of access without use: a tool people can open and mostly don't. Two or three sentences on what was switched on and what happened next are enough.

## What You'll Learn

After this module, you will be able to:

- **Interview** for the job a teammate is trying to get done before deciding what to share
- **Choose** among the four sharing strategies against that teammate's outcome
- **Find** the absorption bottleneck and draft a technical plan and a people plan side by side
- **Test** the assumptions a switch depends on and name the first person to talk to

## Key Concepts

- Interview for the teammate's job before choosing what to share.
- Four sharing strategies: share the context, share a skill, share the output (push), share an interface (pull).
- Push and pull deliver a running agent's value without the teammate running one.
- Pick the candidate against the teammate's outcome; infrastructure is a constraint, not a reason.
- The absorption bottleneck is the obstacle that keeps several others hard.
- The people plan carries the same weight as the technical plan.
- Test the switch, not the solution.
- Access is a switch someone flips. Absorption happens in other people's weeks, at their pace, for their reasons.
