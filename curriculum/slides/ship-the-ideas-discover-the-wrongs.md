## It is not about you being right
<!--slide:ship-the-ideas-discover-the-wrongs-->

I have learned more than half what this is all about by my mistakes and attempts. Acting creates the visceral proof whether or not something is real.

Bit by bit, my brain developed the neural pathways. I could put 1+1 together and see where this all is likely going. Not in detail, but I could still see the patterns.

It is not about you being right.

Antti Tevanlinna

When the digest agrees with you, that is the moment to go looking for your wrong.
