## Specifying what to build is now the harder half
<!--slide:you-specify-it-builds-->

An agent can quickly build a small, testable version of an idea: a clickable flow, a landing page that offers a feature before it exists, a short survey. It builds what the specification says, and the specification comes from you.

Specifying well is the harder half: which customer, which step of their journey, what they should be able to do at the end, and which signal would tell you the idea was wrong.

Nobody can hand the agent that knowledge of your customers and your direction. The three of you write it together: the product owner brings what is worth testing and its signal, the designer the customer's words and journey, the team lead how the team will build it and check it.
