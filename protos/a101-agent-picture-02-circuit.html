PROTO A101-02 THE FULL AGENT PICTURE · CIRCUIT The loop is the shape. Six pieces are stations or walls on it; the human stands on a rail above, with one line down to each piece.

Idea

The loop is the organising shape: context is read, the model generates, tools act, checks select, memory keeps what survived, and the next pass starts sharper.
The goal sits in the middle as what every pass is judged against; the boundary is the wall around the circuit; the interface is its one opening, where the work meets a person.
You are not on the circuit. You stand on a rail one level up, and each of the eight lines down is one decision: what you give that piece, and what you keep back.

What it cannot show

The order you added the pieces in: the figure shows the finished arrangement, not the one-at-a-time build.
That other agents live inside the tools piece, and that memory is context that persists; both are compressed into a verb and a caption.
What any one give/keep-back decision actually looked like; the lines are all the same line.

Where it breaks

Drawing tools after the model on one clockwise track implies a fixed order; a real agent re-enters tools and context many times inside one pass.
The interface also sits where you meet the agent before the work starts, and one exit gate undersells that half.
