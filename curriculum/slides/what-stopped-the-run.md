## An overnight run stops on questions and closed doors
<!--slide:what-stopped-the-run-->

An agent working through the night can stop before the job is done, and its digest may not say so. What to look for when it comes back:

- It waited for you. A choice it would not make alone, asked as a question, with nobody there to answer.
- It hit a source it decided it could not open. A ticket system it had no access to, a missing analytics export. It stopped rather than guess.
- It finished the part it managed. The summary is confident, and the interviews it never reached go unmentioned.

Whether that source was really out of reach is your call, not the agent's.
