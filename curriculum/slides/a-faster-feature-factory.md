## A faster feature factory is still a feature factory
<!--slide:a-faster-feature-factory-->

Agents amplify the way a team already works. They do not transform it.

A team that tests its bets gets to test more of them, sooner. A team that ships without asking whether it worked now ships more of it, faster, and still never finds out. A digest that agrees with the favourite hypothesis keeps that team busy and sure of itself.

Speed shows up in both teams' numbers. The difference is whether anyone checks what the shipped work changed for customers. That check is product craft, and agents do not bring it with them.
