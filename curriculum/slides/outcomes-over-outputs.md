## An outcome is what customers do differently
<!--slide:outcomes-over-outputs-->

Josh Seiden separates what a team makes from what changes. The output is what you ship. In a 2026 piece he defines the outcome as "a measurable change in human behavior that creates value".

His warning: a product can meet every requirement and pass every test, and still create no value. Value appears only when someone uses it to do something new, or to do something they already do in a better way.

So name the outcome. Not "adds a dashboard", but what a named customer will be doing differently once it works. His piece has the agent angle in its title: AI makes the output, you own the outcome.
