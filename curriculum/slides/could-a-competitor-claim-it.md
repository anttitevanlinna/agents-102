## A line a competitor could claim is still generic
<!--slide:could-a-competitor-claim-it-->

Read what your team wrote about the product one line at a time, and ask: could a competitor claim this?

"Saves your team time." Anyone can print that. "Product managers stop rebuilding the quarterly report from four spreadsheets." That one needs someone who has listened to your customers.

Mark every line a competitor could claim. Then ask what you know that they don't: a quote from an interview, a pattern in the tickets, a number from your own analytics.

The same test works on anything an agent drafts for you.
