# Exercise: Find your two crux

**Time:** 25 minutes.

**What you do:**

Your memory now holds a week of answers, your first move and a peer's outside view. Find the two obstacles that, once they move, make the rest movable.

## Find the candidates

Find the crux of your team's move toward agentic work, from everything in the memory.

{{prompt:em-find-two-crux}}

## Push back until each one names a mechanism

Cut any candidate that is a goal in disguise or a category. "Adoption is slow" is a symptom: ask what makes it slow on your team, and whose answer shows it. Keep pushing until each candidate is a sentence you could say to the people it involves.

## Pick two

Pick the two that would release the most if they moved. Claude writes them into Team Knowledge as hypotheses tagged `crux` and logs the choice, with the candidates you cut, in the Decision Journal. If either crux needs more than one sentence, send it back.

## Design (EM proving run 2026-09-30)

- **Atomic — no phase markers.** one conversation with the crux prompt; push-back and pick happen inside it.
- **Role in M3:** the module's major decision; the Big Idea lands here.
- **Prompt:** `em-find-two-crux`. Crux method inlined (no crux skill ships to this audience; Agents 101 teaches it as `exercises/name-your-crux.md`). Goal / category / mechanism test and the three-things-release test sit in the fence (prompts §34). 4–6 candidates with quoted evidence (prompts §13) and the alternative each beats; push-back rewrites, not defends (prompts §18); pick asked in chat.
- **Tier (decision 2026-09-30):** crux enter Team Knowledge as hypotheses tagged `crux`, not rules. One week of evidence is a hypothesis; M1's tiering holds, and the weekly diagnostic can promote or demote them. M4 reads `crux`-tagged hypotheses.
- **Push-back section:** body steers the push-back (pedagogy §62) and supplies no crux (pedagogy §53).
- **Consumed by:** `em-schedule-coalition-checkin`, `em-close-find-the-two-crux`, M4 `em-deliberate-intent`, `em-rehearse-reactions`, M4 Start here.
- **Leap test (§45), by next working day:**
  1. Can say both crux aloud in one sentence each, without opening the file.
  2. Has a Decision Journal entry naming at least two candidate crux not picked and why.
  3. Raises one crux with a coalition member and records their reaction as an observation in `observations/`.
