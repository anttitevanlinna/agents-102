## Run more than one check on your own material
<!--slide:run-more-than-one-check-->

Someone will tell you the fix for invented claims: make the agent cite its sources, or have a second agent with a different brief read its work. You try one, it catches some things, and what it missed stays out of sight.

You do not know in advance which method catches what in your interviews and tickets. Nobody does. So do not pick one on how it sounds.

Seed a research summary with a few claims you know are false. Run several methods on it side by side. Count which one found which false claim, which ones it walked past, and which true lines it flagged. Keep the one that caught most of them without false alarms, with a note of what it missed.
