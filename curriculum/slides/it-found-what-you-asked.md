## It found what you asked it to look for
<!--slide:it-found-what-you-asked-->

Your look-for line asked the agent to find what supports your hypothesis. It did as asked. Ask an agent to look for something in enough customer conversations and it will usually find a few. Your line never asked how many customers said something else, or what would show the hypothesis wrong.

The agreement came from your question, not from your customers. Ask again the other way round: what in this material argues against our hypothesis, and how much of it is there?
