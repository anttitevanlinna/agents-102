## Three walls stand between your laptops and the team
<!--slide:three-walls-->

A setup that works for three people meets the company here:

- Data access: can the agents read the interviews, tickets and analytics the rest of the team works from, and who approves that?
- Where it runs: a setup on your own laptop answers to you. A team's agents need a place the company has approved.
- Whether anyone finds it: a weekly digest nobody knows about changes nothing.

Your company will hit one of these first. A proposal to the wider team names which one. A proposal with no wall in it was written about a laptop.
