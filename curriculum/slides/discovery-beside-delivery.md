## Discovery runs beside delivery, every week
<!--slide:discovery-beside-delivery-->

The 2020 Scrum Guide is about delivery: "The entire Scrum Team is accountable for creating a valuable, useful Increment every Sprint." It names three accountabilities, the Developers, the Product Owner and the Scrum Master, in one self-managing team. The Sprint Goal is "a commitment by the Developers", agreed by the whole team.

Teresa Torres describes the other half. Continuous discovery is "weekly touch points with customers, by the team building the product, where they conduct small research activities, in pursuit of a desired product outcome."

Same team, same weeks, two tracks side by side. A proposal for the wider team says where the agents' weekly work sits: digesting new interviews and tickets for discovery, or helping deliver the Sprint Goal, and who reads what each one produces.
