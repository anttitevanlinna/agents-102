## Future leadership is about strategy, outcomes and customers
<!--slide:future-leadership-->

When building took most of a team's weeks, leading meant rationing that capacity: who builds what, in which order, by when. When building is cheap, that rationing matters less.

What stays is what nobody can hand the agent. Strategy: which outcome is worth the team's year. Outcomes: whether customers now do something differently. Customers: what they actually said and did, heard first-hand.

That is leadership for each of the three of you, not only for whoever holds the title. A team lead whose week went into coordinating the build gets a different job: keeping the team's choices clear enough that people and agents can act on them.
