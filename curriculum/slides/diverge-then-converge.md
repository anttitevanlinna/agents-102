## An agent widens in minutes; the choosing stays with you
<!--slide:diverge-then-converge-->

An agent can widen a list of opportunities, or of rough solutions, in minutes. Choosing between them is still yours, and it goes better when widening and choosing stay apart. Choose while the list is still growing, and the first plausible option tends to win.

The UK Design Council's double diamond draws that rhythm twice, once for the problem and once for the solution: explore widely, then narrow. In the Council's words, "This is not a linear process": what a team learns can send it back to the beginning.
