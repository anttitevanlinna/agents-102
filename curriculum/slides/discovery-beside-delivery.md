## Discovery runs beside delivery, every week
<!--slide:discovery-beside-delivery-->

The 2020 Scrum Guide holds the whole team accountable for "a valuable, useful Increment every Sprint". Learning about customers does not have to wait until that work is done.

In 2007 Desirée Sy described how her user experience team at Alias ran design and usability testing in a parallel track, at least one cycle ahead of the developers, who received designs already tested with users. Designers and developers talked every day.

Continuous discovery, in Teresa Torres's sense, takes the same rhythm further, to what is worth building at all: the team that builds stays in touch with customers every week. The agents' weekly work can sit on either side: digesting new interviews and tickets ahead of the build, or helping deliver the Sprint Goal.
