## You steer the agent in three ways
<!--slide:three-ways-to-steer-->

- By what you bring. The agent amplifies the stance you take. A stated doubt, a standard the answer has to clear, or asking for what is wrong before what is right changes what comes back.
- By what you set around it. A check, or a second read with a different question. Each sits outside the conversation and catches what a chain of mostly-right steps lets through.
- By what you ask for. The shape of the answer: a ranked list with a source for each line, a one-page box, three options side by side. Tell it what you want.

None of the three needs technical skill. Each needs someone who knows the product.
