## Agree what would kill the idea before you test it
<!--slide:the-test-that-could-kill-it-->

Alex Osterwalder's Test Card (Strategyzer, 2015) makes four things explicit before a test: what needs to be true for the idea to work, how you will test it, what you will measure, and the threshold that counts as success. Write the threshold down before the results come in, while nobody has a favourite yet. Below it, the hypothesis fails.

Pick the cheapest test that could prove it wrong. *Testing Business Ideas* (David Bland and Osterwalder, 2019) collects over forty experiments, among them Concierge, Wizard of Oz, Simple Landing Page and Feature Stub.

Agents can build a landing page in an afternoon. The card keeps a cheap test honest: the line was drawn before anyone saw the numbers.
