## A feature factory ships without asking if it worked
<!--slide:the-feature-factory-->

In a feature factory, deciding what to build gets rigour and finding out whether it worked gets none. Nobody measures the impact. Nobody compares the benefit expected with the benefit delivered. "Developers have 'passing tests', but product managers do not."

Those are signs from John Cutler's list, in a November 2016 post. He traced the term to a developer friend who was "just sitting in the factory, cranking out features, and sending them down the line."

His own caveat, three years later: a prescriptive "build this" bet may be the right approach. The term names missing validation, not a team that ships a lot.
