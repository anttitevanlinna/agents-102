## A tidy tree looks decided even when nobody chose
<!--slide:structure-is-persuasive-->

An opportunity tree with neat branches, or a plan with headings and bold text, looks like a decision. Often it is a draft formatted like one. An agent produces tidy structure in seconds, so tidiness tells you nothing about whether anyone chose.

Two more pressures ride along. Each step sounds reasonable, so the whole sounds right. But reasonable steps with one missing still lead to the wrong bet. Judge what they add up to.

And when the plan matches what you would have written, it feels like agreement. It was written from a partial read of your evidence. Read it assuming something in it is wrong. There usually is.
