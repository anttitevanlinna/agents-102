**Status:** v0.2 — 2026-09-24. Placement shipped on branch `a101-story` (story blend, 2026-09-23: sycophancy and the self-report at the M1 close, beige and the frame at M3, the braid law and Bainbridge at M6, Polanyi at M7, the future question at M8) and the laws that lived only in module Key Concepts moved into their lectures (2026-09-24); Key Concepts are now glance lists. The full agent picture is drawn (`curriculum/figures/agent-picture.md`, shown once at the M6 close). Open: the narrator's scar (`pre-cohort-todos.md` § 9); earned-not-announced, still capped by the Big Idea openers. v0.1 (2026-09-22) was enumeration only; the placement table below is kept as the record of what was proposed. Sibling of the
AE101 plan at repo-root `theory-plan.md`, same frame, different audience.

**Why this exists.** The step-0 story-depth run (`curriculum/evals/story-depth.md`) found A101's
arcs enacted and unnamed: six developed learnings carried by exercises, the Debrief ritual and
the mood contract, with the name arriving late (M6's recap) or never. Borrowed theory sits on
the leadership side (JTBD, Martin, Rumelt, Klein, Mollick, two court-record cases) and is nearly
absent on the agent side — nothing names why the self-report is fluent-but-wrong, why trust
decays as it is earned, why three stances beat one summarizer. The creativity beats are on the
page and no cold reader named them as a learning, because nothing braids them. Forward-looking
is the lowest factor (44): three orphan questions, none echoed. AE101 fixed the same two
failures — no spine, no durable body — with a map and per-module law anchors. A101 needs the
same, for a leader.

**Working headline (Antti):** *controlling your agent and making your agents creative is one
act of leadership.* Floor and ceiling, set by different instruments, both built by the leader.
The closer already says it (`new-human-role-in-the-loop`: *"Groundedness protects the floor.
Steering raises the ceiling"*). The plan below is organised around that pair.

## What "theory" means here

Same frame as AE101 — theory is the generating function behind the moves; it pays three rents
(predicts · generates the next move · grounded); it reaches the student derived or as a
governor, never front-loaded; the field is theory-borrowing, not theory-less. One addition for
this audience: **a leader's theory must generate a decision, not only a move.** The engineer's
governor fires before a session; the leader's fires in a meeting where someone else will do the
work. Every piece below is tested on that — *does it change what the leader decides on Tuesday
about agents they will never personally run?*

The analogy the headline rests on — running agents is running an organisation — earns its keep
where it **breaks**, and the breaks are A101's hardest lessons: the agent is the colleague who
always agrees (sycophancy); it does not learn between Tuesdays unless the loop was built; it
has no accountability, so the decision stays human; it reads every document as an instruction
(prompt injection — no employee does that). Each break is a self-challenging beat waiting to be
named.

### 1 · Laws of the machine — what a leader must know about the thing they are delegating to

- ★ **Agreeable answers won the preference round (sycophancy).** The model mirrors the stance
  you bring because mirroring was rewarded in tuning; the self-report is the same kind of
  output. `[borrow:Sharma et al. 2023, "Towards Understanding Sycophancy"]` `[owed]`
  `[curr:getting-going Debrief]` — enacted (*"the LLM is generous to a rules file it just helped
  write"*, *"warm-session self-audit"*), never named. AE101 names it at the M1 close; A101 can
  name it at the same beat.
- ★ **The self-report is a hypothesis, not ground truth.** `[curr:getting-going cold critic;
  module-5-prework "asking the same model 'are you sure?' is another fluent answer"]` The
  strongest A101 learning in step 0 (confident ≠ verified, depth 85–100); the law under it is
  unnamed until M5.
- ★ **Compound-reliability floor: mostly-right steps stack to mostly-wrong.** `[rsch:L3]`
  `[curr:grounded.md — 85% × 10 steps]` Named once at M5, not echoed. The single number a CTO
  takes home; earns a place in the spine.
- **Non-determinism: same prompt, same files, different session, different answer.**
  `[curr:practice-of-risk]` Named at M4 as one of three ways agents break the classical
  security story. Consequence never drawn for evals: one session is a sample (AE101 §2 has it;
  A101's M6 fixed-judge loop enacts it without saying so).
- **Well-formed English is the attack surface (prompt injection as a class).**
  `[curr:security KC, practice-of-risk]` `[borrow:Willison, the lethal trifecta]` `[owed]`
  Named and developed at M4; the analogy break (*no employee reads every document as an
  instruction*) is the leader-shaped restatement, unused.
- **Context is the model the agent acts from.** `[curr:context-is-king, compounding]` A101's
  founding idea, developed prompt → memory → shared context. The scoped Good Regulator borrow
  from AE101 applies with the same caveat (why steering works at all; never proof a file is
  required). `[borrow:Conant–Ashby, scoped]`
- **Text becomes action.** An agent action starts as words another system obeys; wrong output
  becomes wrong action. `[curr:multi-agent-systems opener, output-quality, evaluations
  callout]` Stated three times as a transition, never as a law. Candidate for the floor.

### 2 · Laws of control — the floor

- ★ **Certainty is unavailable; a repeatable loop replaces it, and the residual is an artefact.**
  Assess → mitigate → reassess residual → decide; *"I can't tell"* is a real answer.
  `[curr:practice-of-risk, security]` `[borrow:risk management — residual-risk vocabulary;
  medicine ("safe enough, under these conditions, for now")]` A101's second-strongest learning
  (depth 85–100). Already named; owes its position on a spine, not more words.
- ★ **Verification must leave the generation loop.** A check inside the same window inherits
  the same framing; the check touches the source or it is not a check. `[curr:module-5-prework,
  grounded, hallucination-bakeoff]` Named at M5. Same law as AE101's *independence* clause in
  `the-gate-is-a-claim`; A101 has the law, not the recursion below.
- **The gate is a claim too.** Green means what the gate can see; judges miscalibrate, gates
  get gamed, sessions get lucky. `[borrow:Goodhart]` `[curr:evaluations KC "a yardstick you
  rewrite is not a yardstick"; when-the-score-stops-moving]` A101 has the fixed-yardstick half
  and the *what the judge can't see* half; it never turns the scrutiny on the check as a claim.
  The one control law A101 lacks outright.
- **Calibrate the judge against your own judgement.** `[borrow:Husain 2024]` `[owed]` AE101
  has it; A101's M5 scoreboard enacts it (four detectors adjudicated against sources) and the
  move is never generalised to *your* judgement vs the judge's.
- ★ **Autonomy is earned rung by rung; the rung is a property of the action, not the agent.**
  `[curr:what-is-an-agent § autonomy ladder; output-quality § Agent Actions "which rung has
  this action earned"]` The governor A101 uses most; the ladder lives in a supplementary and is
  reached by link.
- **Propose, double-check, apply.** `[curr:output-quality]` The leader's HITL placement: the
  expert reviews a proposal with evidence and a known limit, never a blank page. Named once,
  refires at M6's callout. Governor-shaped already.
- **Principal–agent.** Delegated authority + hidden action + goal misalignment; "agent" is the
  literal economic relationship. `[borrow:economics — Jensen & Meckling 1976]` `[owed]` Not on
  any A101 surface. For a leader this is the most native borrow in the plan: they already run
  principal–agent problems with people, and the agent has *worse* incentives (it optimises for
  the gate) and *better* observability (the transcript). Candidate for M4 or M6.
- **Scope beats patch; the best mitigation is the door you don't open.** `[curr:practice-of-
  risk]` `[borrow:least privilege]` Named at M4, governor-shaped (*should the agent have write
  access to that system?*).
- ★ **Trust and vigilance move in opposite directions.** Each clean task is real evidence of
  competence and real erosion of your watching. `[borrow:Bainbridge 1983, Ironies of
  Automation; Parasuraman & Riley 1997, use/misuse/disuse]` `[owed]` Not on any A101 surface.
  The counter-voice *the human moves one level up* lacks (self-challenging 20–40 in step 0):
  moving up a level is exactly where the watching stops. Candidate for the M6 closer's *would
  you let it send the mail?* beat.
- **Blast radius: grant autonomy where the downside is bounded and reversible.** `[borrow:SRE]`
  `[curr:security — scope/split/filter/gate/review]` Enacted as the mitigation menu, not named
  as the sizing rule. Governor: *what's the worst thing it could do with that access?* is
  already A101's M3→M4 bridge.

### 3 · Laws of creativity — the ceiling

The half the step-0 cold reads could not name. Every beat exists; no law does.

- ★ **Requisite variety: three stances beat one summarizer; the synthesizer averages to beige.** A controller's repertoire must match the variety of what it steers; one head, one
  pass, one flat take. `[borrow:Ashby 1956]` `[curr:multi-agent-systems KC, three-minds-one-
  synthesis, when-to-split-an-agent]` The clearest creativity law in the corpus, enacted at M3
  with a named failure mode (*beige*) and no name for the mechanism. Also the split rule:
  splitting earns its keep only where the agents genuinely can't be one (access, dialect,
  stance) — variety you cannot fake with one prompt.
- ★ **A framework makes it pick.** Without a spine the synthesizer summarises; with one it
  chooses. `[curr:multi-agent-systems KC "frameworks are the synthesizer's spine"]`
  `[borrow:Rumelt 2011, the kernel — diagnosis · guiding policy · coherent actions]` Named at
  M3, refires M8. The leader's version of *your stance is the ceiling*: the frame you hand the
  agent sets what it can conclude.
- **Groundedness protects the floor; steering raises the ceiling.** Two evals, two jobs; do
  not collapse them. `[curr:new-human-role-in-the-loop, evals-as-steering]` The headline in the
  training's own words, at M6, unnamed as the spine. This is where the two halves of the plan
  meet on the page.
- **Deliberate variance: the Rory seat.** A stance whose job is to question the obvious take
  and steal an analogy from an unrelated field — variance injected on purpose, then selected
  against. `[curr:three-minds-one-synthesis]` `[grnd — house move]` Enacted, unnamed as a
  mechanism.
- **Exploration and exploitation compete for the same capacity.** `[borrow:March 1991]`
  `[owed]` Not on any surface. The org-science name for the headline's tension: control is
  exploitation, creativity is exploration, and a system tuned hard to one starves the other.
  Candidate for the spine's one-line grounding, design-side only.
- ★ **Variation → selection → retention.** Generate variants, let a judge select, let memory
  retain. `[borrow:Campbell 1960, BVSR]` `[curr:hallucination-bakeoff (four candidates, one
  scorer, keep the winner); eval-loop (generator varies, fixed judge selects, tactic file
  retains)]` **The braid law.** Creativity and control are not two instruments in tension but
  two stages of one mechanism, and A101 runs the whole cycle twice (M5, M6) without naming it.
  If the headline needs one law under it, this is the candidate.
- **Diverge, then converge; assumption-test and pre-mortem.** `[borrow:Design Council double
  diamond; Martin — what would have to be true; Klein 2007 — pre-mortem]` `[curr:joint-double-
  diamond, test-the-sharing-plan, design-the-sharing-plan]` Named inline at M7–M8 as
  disciplines. Already attributed on the page; owes only the connection to the ceiling.
- **Garbage can vs bitter lesson.** Does defining the outcome and letting the model find the
  path beat mapping the organisational mess? `[borrow:Cohen, March & Olsen 1972 via Mollick;
  Sutton 2019]` `[curr:evaluations opener, M6 prework]` The one forward-looking question A101
  asks well, and drops. Belongs in §4 as the future thread; listed here because it is *also*
  the creativity question — how much path-finding to leave to the agent.

### 4 · Laws of compounding and the organisation — the future thread

- ★ **Persistence + automation = system; the system gets better by being edited.**
  `[curr:compounding, building-agent-systems KC, how-do-you-make-your-system-learn]` A101's
  founding compounding law, named at M2.
- **Double-loop learning.** Single-loop fixes the output; double-loop changes the rule that
  let it recur. `[borrow:Argyris & Schön 1974/1978]` `[owed]` `[curr: every Debrief — "do the
  work → capture the rules → reflect → sharpen"]` Enacted eight times, never named. The
  Debrief ritual is A101's most-repeated move and its meaning changes twice (M6: the loop
  sharpens without you; M8: agents build agents); naming it once would convert repetition
  into development.
- ★ **The model rotates; the organisation's learning rate compounds — or doesn't.** The bitter
  lesson read for organisations: what you built around the model is superseded; what the
  organisation learned about building around models is not. `[borrow:Sutton 2019]`
  `[grnd:learning-rate ceiling]` `[curr:evaluations opener (Mollick), agents-building-agents
  "flywheel, not a graduation"]` The future thread A101 needs: a question, not a prediction —
  *will your organisation learn faster than the model changes underneath it?* — seeded early,
  echoed at M6 and M8. Today it appears once at M6 and is dropped.
- ★ **Absorptive capacity.** An organisation's ability to recognise, assimilate and apply new
  knowledge is a function of what it already knows — you cannot absorb what you have no prior
  competence to receive. `[borrow:Cohen & Levinthal 1990]` `[owed]` `[curr:personal-to-team
  "access is not absorption"; agents-building-agents three walls]` The canonical theory behind
  A101's best leader idea, and the reason the idea is a one-module headline today: it has no
  law to travel on. Also explains *competence-first* from the outside — the org can only absorb
  agents in proportion to the competence it already holds. Candidate for M7's law anchor, with
  a plant at M2 (the memory is the *student's* absorptive capacity, made literal).
- **Access–trust gap.** Experimentation widespread, production trust rare, gap always large.
  `[rsch:L4 per strategy doc]` `[curr:personal-to-team; supplementary/personal-to-company-gap
  — the 54–95% / 5–22% figures sit on that page flagged SOURCE NEEDED]` Check level against
  `continuous-research` before any stamp — the sibling absorption-bottleneck finding was
  knocked L4 → L2 on 2026-07-31 for URL-less sources, and this one is a student surface
  carrying an unsourced statistic today.
- **Absorption bottleneck.** Once generation is cheap, the binding constraint is the human
  evaluation rate. `[rsch:L2 — Osmani direct; kb file downgraded]`
  `[curr:supplementary/personal-to-company-gap — Osmani *Software Factories* and Ronacher
  *The Coming Loop* both linked; personal-to-team KC "the technical plan fills quickly; the
  people plan stalls on names"]` The sources are already on an A101 surface; the law is
  named in a supplementary no module links: `personal-to-company-gap.md` is absent from the
  `TRAININGS` registry in `site/layouts/curriculum.js` (as is `agent-ready-data.md`), so it
  renders nowhere a student is sent. Registering it is a delivery decision, not a theory one. For a leader the
  bottleneck is organisational (who reads, who decides, who signs), which is where §5's role
  shift lands.
- **We know more than we can tell (tacit knowledge).** `[borrow:Polanyi 1966]`
  `[curr:supplementary/personal-to-company-gap, quoted]` The leader's version of AE101's
  *your frontier is what you have expressed*: the agent runs on what has been written down, and
  most of what a leader knows about their organisation has not been. Explains why the memory
  (M2) is the work and why sharing context (M7) is harder than sharing a file. On the page
  once, in an unlinked supplementary.
- **Three walls after competence: data access, runtime platform, discoverability.**
  `[rsch:L2–3, F-Secure evidence]` `[curr:agents-building-agents KC]` Named at M8.
- **Amplification; individual ≠ institutional.** AI amplifies the system it enters; personal
  gains do not aggregate without organisational change. `[rsch:L3]` Not on any A101 surface;
  the leader-facing consequence of *cross personal → team*. Candidate for M7.
- **Jobs to be done; the replacement test.** `[borrow:Christensen; Martin — what would have to
  be true for them to replace it?]` `[curr:share-your-work, personal-to-team KC]` Named and
  attributed at M7. Adoption theory the leader already owns; the plan changes nothing here.
- **Compound interest on a rules file.** `[curr:getting-going "compound interest on a rule
  file"]` `[borrow:finance, by analogy]` Named at M1 and M2. Fine as a picture; not a law.

### 5 · The human's position — the leader's arc

- ★ **The human moves one level up: from inside every loop to deciding the shape of the loop.**
  `[curr:new-human-role-in-the-loop — the M1–M6 role recap]` Named at M6, paid off at M8.
  Control-theory grounding, design-side: the leader stops being the controller and becomes the
  designer of the setpoint (what "better" means), the sensors (what gets checked), and the
  autonomy boundary (what it may reach). Jargon stays here; the student meets *what stays
  fixed, what may change, what gets checked every time*.
- **The new role's failure mode is Bainbridge's.** Moving up a level is where the reps stop;
  the leader who designs the loop and never runs one loses the ability to tell wrong from
  right at a glance. `[borrow:Bainbridge]` The counter-voice §2 lists; placed here because it
  is *this* learning's doubt. Governor: *when did you last do this by hand?* — AE101's line,
  leader-shaped as *when did you last read one of these unprompted?*
- **The decision stays human because the agent has no accountability.** `[curr:joint-double-
  diamond "the agents produced the options; the human picks"; agents-building-agents
  identity close]` Enacted at M8; the law (accountability cannot be delegated, only authority
  can) is the principal–agent borrow's second half. `[borrow:economics]`

### 6 · Governors — the pre-action questions a leader carries

Each is theory in operation; the test is that it fires in a meeting about agents the leader
will not run. Those marked `[curr]` are already on the page; the others are the gaps.

- **Which rung has this action earned?** `[curr:output-quality]` — autonomy is per action.
- **What's the worst thing it could do with that access?** `[curr:M3 → M4 bridge]` — blast
  radius before capability.
- **Propose, double-check, apply — where does the human sit?** `[curr:output-quality]`
- **"I can't tell" — what would close it?** `[curr:security reflection]` — residual as artefact.
- **Would you let it send the mail? What would have to be true?** `[curr:new-human-role]` —
  the whole arc as one decision.
- **Could a competitor claim this?** `[curr:compounding]` — the genericness test on memory;
  generalises to any context the leader funds.
- **What would have to be true? Pre-mortem it.** `[curr:M7–M8, attributed]`
- **Who absorbs this, and what do they already know?** — absorptive capacity as a question.
  Not on the page; the leader's version of *who reads the output*.
- **What would change our mind?** — the forward-looking top rung; no A101 learning reaches it.
  Not on the page anywhere.
- **When did you last do this by hand?** — Bainbridge as a question. Not on the page.
- **Is this rules-based, verifiable, talent-constrained?** `[rsch:L4 convergence triad]` The
  go/no-go for which process to try first; the strategy doc's own candidate for M8's diamond.

**Caveats.** A borrowed piece earns its keep only if the training can show a scar it walked —
otherwise it is analogy-theatre, and the leader audience is faster to smell it than the
engineer. House-thesis pieces (learning-rate ceiling, competence-first, the headline itself)
carry confirmation-bias risk; the step-0 rubric's *self-challenging* factor is the adversarial
check, not a formality. Research-tagged levels are as the strategy doc recorded them and two
have moved since; re-verify before any stamp.

## The spine — candidate, not decided

**Found after this section was drafted (2026-09-23, quartet run):** A101 already runs a frame,
unnamed by author or plan — the *full agent picture* refrain, one anatomical piece per module
(context → shelf life → tools/other agents → boundary → checks → loop → interface), stated in
the same construction in at least six lectures. Two of three storytelling judges found it
independently; the third read the frame as *context: what you feed, what you withhold, who
owns the gap*. The floor/ceiling candidate below is a claim about what that anatomy is *for*;
the two are compatible, and the training currently says neither out loud. Any spine decision
starts from the refrain that is on the page.

AE101's spine is a map of phases. A101's audience does not run phases; they set floors and
ceilings on work other people and agents do. Candidate spine: **two instruments on one loop** — the floor (what must be true before output becomes action) and the ceiling (what
makes the output worth acting on) — with the loop between them the thing the leader designs.

| | Floor — control | Ceiling — creativity | Loop — what compounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Law | residual-risk loop · verification leaves the loop · the gate is a claim · autonomy per action | requisite variety · a framework makes it pick · deliberate variance · steering raises the ceiling | persistence + automation · double-loop · VSR · absorptive capacity · the model rotates |
| Failure the leader has seen | Avianca: fluent, wrong, acted on | beige: three views averaged into none | the demo that never reached Monday |
| Governor | which rung has this earned? | what would have to be true? | who absorbs this? what would change our mind? |
| Braid | | | VSR is where floor and ceiling are one mechanism: variety is generated, the floor selects, memory retains |

Where the borrow breaks, stated on the design side: control and creativity in organisations
are a *tension* (March); in the VSR cycle they are *stages*. Both readings are true at
different zooms, and the student surface should carry the felt tension (beige vs Avianca) and
let the mechanism arrive at M6 where the training already runs the full cycle.

Two findings fall out of the assembly, as they did for AE101. **The ceiling column is A101's uncredited strength** — M3, M5 and M6 all run variety-then-selection, and no reader named it
because it is never called anything. **The future thread is the loop column's missing echo** —
*the model rotates* is the question the whole training is an answer to, and it is asked once.

## Placement — candidates against the mood arc

Doctrine as AE101's: name after the move; container may front-load; the mood contract
(`bosser-strategy:content-strategy.md` § *The mood arc*) is the constraint — no naming beat
may resolve a doubt the next module needs. All rows are candidates; each becomes a card when
it touches student-facing text.

| Module | Mood | Naming beat (after the move) | Piece |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 close | joy | machine-nature closer, after the exercises, as AE101's `the-machine-you-just-met` does: why the retro was generous | sycophancy · self-report as hypothesis |
| M1 open | joy | move *Context is King* to after the first guardrail attempt | earned-not-announced fix (judge 3's smallest move) |
| M2 close | compounding | one line naming the Debrief as double-loop; plant *the model rotates* as a question | double-loop · future thread seed |
| M3 close | unease — do NOT resolve | name variety and beige as a mechanism; keep the doubt | requisite variety · a framework makes it pick |
| M4 | deepened unease | already named; add the analogy break (*no employee reads every document as an instruction*) | principal–agent (optional) |
| M5 | rescue, bounded | already named; add compound-reliability as the spine's number | verification leaves the loop |
| M6 close | leverage | the two-evals slide becomes the spine reveal; Bainbridge under *would you let it send the mail?*; echo *the model rotates* | floor/ceiling · trust vs vigilance · VSR named |
| M7 | generosity | one law under *access is not absorption*; plant it at M2 (the memory as your own absorptive capacity) | absorptive capacity |
| M8 | awe, forward hunger | callback *access is not absorption* against the shared folder; close on the open question, not the flywheel's certainty | learning-rate question · *what would change our mind?* |

Dosage: ≤3 named laws per module, one spine picture shown twice (M6 reveal, M8 echo), theory
as container not content. The extension modules (M7–M8) are optional in delivery, so the
spine must be whole at M6 and the future thread must have landed by then.

## Gaps this enumeration exposes

1. No creativity law is named anywhere; the beats are the training's best material.
2. No law under *access is not absorption*; absorptive capacity is the obvious one and it is
   forty years old.
3. *The human moves one level up* has no counter-voice; Bainbridge is it.
4. The future is asked once (Mollick at M6) and never echoed; the learning-rate question is
   the training's own thesis and the student never hears it.
5. The agent-side laws (sycophancy, self-report, compound reliability, non-determinism) are
   enacted at M1 and named, if at all, at M4–M5 — three modules of felt experience with no
   handle.
6. Every module announces its thesis before the exercise; the fix is placement, not prose.
