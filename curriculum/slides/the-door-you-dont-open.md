## The best mitigation is the door you don't open
<!--slide:the-door-you-dont-open-->

Before the agent reads anything, decide what it never gets to read. The interview notes with customers' names. The ticket export with phone numbers. The folder nobody remembers sharing.

Every source you leave out is one you never have to explain, clean up or apologise for. Ask of each: does the agent need this to do the job, or is it just there?

It will cost you a source somebody wanted in. Leaving it out is still the right call, and an agent that reaches less is easier for the whole team to trust.
