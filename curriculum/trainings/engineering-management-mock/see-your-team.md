# See your team

## Big Idea

Your knowledge of your people is the input nobody else has. Put it where an agent can work with it.

## What You'll Learn

After this module, you will be able to:

- **Install** a leadership memory with three blocks: Team Knowledge, Decision Journal, Quality Gate
- **Place** each person on ADKAR and your team on the adoption curve, with the evidence for each call

## Diagnose your team

Save your own notes on your team in the folder as `team-notes.md`. A few plain lines per person is enough.

Ask Claude to set up your leadership memory and run the first diagnostic from those notes.
