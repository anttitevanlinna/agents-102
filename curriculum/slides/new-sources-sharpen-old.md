## New interviews sharpen the old insight pages
<!--slide:new-sources-sharpen-old-->

When new interviews arrive, an agent keeping the team's insight pages does not start over. It reads the pages it already keeps first, then the new material, then rewrites.

The old claims and the new ones meet. A pain point heard once gets a second voice, or loses its place. A claim that cannot survive the meeting gets cut. That is how the pages get sharper instead of longer.

What this round reads is what the last round wrote, and the next round reads what this one writes.

Read the cuts too. A claim the agent dropped is a decision someone on the team should see.
