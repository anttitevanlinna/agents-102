## Professional role

A natural person using an AI system as part of a regular freelance or business
activity is a deployer rather than a purely personal user. This establishes the
actor's role. It does not by itself classify every use as high-risk.

## Text publication

The deployer-side disclosure described for generated or manipulated text is
tied to publication intended to inform the public on matters of public interest
without human review or editorial control. The guidance discusses scope and
exceptions, including ordinary editing. Evidence of real editorial
responsibility therefore matters to the classification of the publishing path.

## Other content

Separate transparency rules concern deepfakes, emotion recognition, biometric
categorisation, and direct interaction with an AI system. The current inventory
contains none of those uses. Adding a chatbot or synthetic person would require
a new assessment rather than reuse of today's conclusion.

## Application to this fixture

Salla is a professional deployer. Her use inventory is limited to non-clinical
drafting and editing, followed by human review. That evidence cuts against the
outside checklist's claim that her title alone makes every use high-risk or that
every assisted sentence necessarily requires a label. It does not prove blanket
exemption, settle whether a particular psychology publication concerns a matter
