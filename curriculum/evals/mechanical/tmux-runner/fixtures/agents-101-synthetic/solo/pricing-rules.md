# Pilot pricing — account classes

Price: monthly floor plus a per-route rate above the floor's included routes.

- **bill-shock risk:** routes this month are at least 40% above last month AND the account is SMB.
- **watch:** routes moved 20-40% month on month, either direction.
- **expansion:** a mid-market account above its included routes for two months running.
- **steady:** everything else.

Included routes in the floor: SMB 1,000; Mid 6,000.

Open point: the rules do not say which class wins when an account meets two (for example a mid-market account that moved 25%).
