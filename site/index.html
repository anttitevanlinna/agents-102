How it works

Keep the handbook open beside Claude

Each step says what you are about to make. You type a plain sentence into Claude, and the handbook tells you what to look for in the reply. No coding. Take one module a week. On Pro, a long module can reach the usage limit; then you pause and carry on when it resets.

Type in Claude Desktop

List my main meetings this week. Summarize the shape of the week in 5 lines.

In Claude's reply

Your own meetings, read from your calendar or from a screenshot of it, and five lines on what kind of week it is.

What you need

  • Claude Pro or a higher paid plan. Pro is the minimum. The free plan is not enough. See Claude plans
  • A computer. The handbook is written for Claude Desktop on Mac or Windows. It also has versions for Claude Code in a terminal, Codex and GitHub Copilot.
  • A challenge you carry at work that isn't solved yet. Not a task.
  • Connecting work apps is optional. Your calendar and files help, but the course works with screenshots and saved files instead.

What you keep

The system stays in a folder on your computer: the memory of your challenge, your agents, your security skill, your judge and the loop that improves the work.

Next week starts from it, not from zero.
