## Now the move is to give it less
<!--slide:give-it-less-->

You have given the agent more: context, memory, more stances. Now give it less.

More context makes a good answer likelier. Less access makes a bad answer smaller.

Every door you close is a source the agent won't read; every tool you remove is a capability gone. Scope it down far enough and the system is safe and useless. You pick it, write down what is left, and sign for it.
