PROTO A101-07 THE FULL AGENT PICTURE · CLOUD The selection loop, with the model moved to where it lives. Your runtime runs the loop on your machine; each turn, context and goal go up to the model and text comes back.

Idea

The loop runs in your runtime, on your machine, and crosses to the model in the cloud twice a turn: context and the goal go up, text comes back.
The runtime turns that text into tool calls and more than one candidate; the checks pick one against the goal, and what survived is written into context as memory for the next turn.
The wall is the give / keep back decision: it bounds what your runtime may reach, and the cloud sits outside it by nature, so what goes up, goes up.

What it cannot show

That the candidates are often several turns of one model, not one fan in one reply; the fan compresses many round trips.
That the checks come in two kinds (floor and ceiling), and that a check can itself be another call up to a model.
That the interface also carries the goal in and the result out; it is drawn only as the door where a person meets it.

Where it breaks

One gate with one gap implies exactly one winner per turn; real checks often pass none, or several, or hand back to you.
Tool results also go up on the next turn, which the single "context + goal go up" arrow folds in without saying.
