## Slice by what you need to learn
<!--slide:slice-by-what-you-learn-->

Jeff Patton lays the customer's journey left to right as the backbone of a story map, with the details hanging below. Then he cuts horizontal slices across the whole journey, so each release works end to end: in his words, all major features a little at a time, never a car without brakes.

Each slice gets a name for the outcome it should produce and a measure for whether it did. Inside the first release, Patton orders the slices so the team can learn fast and avoid risk.

With agents building, slices are cheap, so the first slice tests the assumption you are least sure of.
