## Talk to customers every week, toward an outcome
<!--slide:talk-to-customers-every-week-->

Teresa Torres defines continuous discovery in one sentence: "weekly touch points with customers, by the team building the product, where they conduct small research activities, in pursuit of a desired product outcome."

Weekly, because saving questions for one big round "overwhelms our customers and it holds up our product decisions." By the team building the product, so the people who decide hear the customer. Small: one interview, one quick test. And toward an outcome: she sees teams that are great at research and still miss their outcomes, because the research served none.

Right after each interview, while the story is fresh, she has the team fill a one-page snapshot built around the story the customer told.

An agent can sort and compare the snapshots. Hearing the customer stays with the team.
