## An outcome is what your customers do differently
<!--slide:outcomes-over-outputs-->

The output is what you ship. The outcome is what changes for the people using it. Josh Seiden, in a 2026 piece, defines the outcome as "a measurable change in human behavior that creates value".

His warning: a product can meet every requirement and pass every test, and still create no value. Value appears only when someone uses it to do something new, or to do something they already do in a better way.

So name the outcome. Not "adds a dashboard", but what a named customer will be doing differently once it works. His title carries the agent angle: AI makes the output, you own the outcome.
