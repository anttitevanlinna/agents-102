## You steer the agent in three ways
<!--slide:three-ways-to-steer-->

- By what you bring. The agent amplifies the stance you take. A stated doubt, a standard the answer has to clear, or asking for what is wrong before what is right changes what comes back.
- By what you set around it. A check, such as a second agent with a different brief. It sits outside the conversation and catches what one pass lets through.
- By what you ask for. The shape of the answer: a ranked list with a source for each line, a one-page summary, three options side by side.

Each needs someone who knows the product.
