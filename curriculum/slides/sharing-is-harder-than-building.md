## We built good things. We failed to share them well.
<!--slide:sharing-is-harder-than-building-->

Sharing is harder than building. We built good things. We failed to share them well. Cross-team knowledge transfer remains the unsolved problem. Agents can dissolve the technical barriers. The social and organisational barriers are still there.

Antti Tevanlinna, *What I Know Now That I Didn't Know Then*, 2026

What the three of you worked out reaches your team only when you plan that step as part of the work.
