## "Are you sure?" is another fluent answer
<!--slide:are-you-sure-->

When you can't tell whether an answer is grounded, asking the agent "Are you sure?" returns another answer from the same source, in the same confident voice.

Large language models generate the next likely word, not the next true one. They are trained on confident, specific, fluent text, so they produce language that looks like that whether or not the material supports it. Fluency is not evidence, and the model cannot tell you which parts of its output are grounded.

Later models will fabricate less; they won't stop.
